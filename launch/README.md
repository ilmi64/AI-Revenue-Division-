# Organisation Launch Readiness Package

This package coordinates ten active divisions for a controlled, evidence-based launch. Animora is on hold and excluded from active implementation.

- [Christmas 2026 / New Year 2027 Fast-Track](CHRISTMAS-2026-FAST-TRACK.md) — readiness by December 24, post-New Year controlled activation planning window, parallel division priorities, Actions release-day sequence and acceptance gates.
- [Organisation Launch Readiness Plan](ORGANISATION-LAUNCH-READINESS-PLAN.md) — two-stage schedule, launch tiers, governance and exit criteria.
- [Division Acceptance Matrix](DIVISION-ACCEPTANCE-MATRIX.md) — current evidence and per-division acceptance gates.
- [Integrated Rehearsal and Operations Runbook](INTEGRATED-REHEARSAL-AND-OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md) — end-to-end scenarios, stop conditions and handovers.
- [Launch Blocker Register](LAUNCH-BLOCKER-REGISTER.md) — current blockers and required closure evidence.
- [Repository Integration Order](REPOSITORY-INTEGRATION-ORDER.md) — reviewed PR dependency relationships and integration cautions.

These documents define a proposed readiness process. They do not establish that tests passed, systems are running, staff are assigned or launch is authorized. Christmas is the readiness deadline; controlled activation is targeted for the first eligible sessions after New Year, subject to venue calendars, evidence and explicit owner approval. Tier B commercial operation and Tier C live financial execution remain separately gated.
