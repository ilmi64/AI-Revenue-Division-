4. **BountyOS — free signal only.** No paid subscription or paywall bypass. The exact task must be independently discoverable from its primary source before consideration.
5. **IssueHunt/Algora old listings — reject unless a current primary record proves reopened and funded.**

## 5. Execution gate for every selected task

Before spending engineering effort, capture:
- exact canonical task URL and same-day timestamp;
- primary sponsor/platform funding confirmation and exact reward currency/amount;
- issue open, unlocked, unassigned or claimable state and competing PR/claim check;
- repo activity, maintainer acceptance authority, acceptance criteria and deadline;
- licence/IP assignment and contributor agreement;
- explicit platform, sponsor and repository AI-assistance rules;
- Ontario/Canada and individual payee eligibility;
- KYC/tax documents, payout rail, minimum, fees, FX and payment timeline;
- probability-adjusted reward, engineering estimate, independent QA effort and expected net value;
- owner approval for any public claim/comment, external code contribution or submission.

If any key term is unknown, status is HOLD. No reward is counted until accepted, awarded and cleared.
