## Free service-marketplace candidates
- **Contra:** free plan permits profile, proposals, payments and getting paid, but job access is limited; its pricing page lists a $15 or $29 client payment fee for payments above $500 on the free plan. Do not upgrade.
  - https://contra.com/pricing
  - https://contra.com/features/find-freelance-jobs
- **Upwork Basic:** $0/month, but currently includes zero monthly Connects by default; users may earn up to 10 monthly through platform tasks. Additional Connects cost USD $0.15 each. Do not buy Connects or subscribe. It is not a reliable zero-spend application route unless free Connects are available.
  - https://www.upwork.com/pricing/freelancer
