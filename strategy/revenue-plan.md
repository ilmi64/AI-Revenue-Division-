# Revenue Plan

## Objective
Build a diversified, low-risk revenue pipeline with AI doing as much production work as possible while keeping human qualification, judgement, and approvals explicit.

## Primary route: AI-coded, funded software work
Prioritise clearly funded, bounded GitHub issues where AI can research, implement, test, document, and prepare a pull request. BOSS is the first platform to investigate because its current documentation lists Canada among supported earning countries and describes bank payouts to individuals. Verify current terms, exact bounty status, and payment conditions for every task. See `operations/ai-coding-bounty-playbook.md`.

## Secondary routes
1. AI-assisted services: narrow, demonstrable deliverables such as ecommerce listing preflight audits and documentation.
2. Reusable digital products: package proven workflows only after demand is evidenced.
3. AI training/evaluation work: lower priority because these tasks often require the owner to personally perform human judgement and qualification.
4. Small AI tools: build only after a specific user problem and free operating path are validated.

## Opportunity scoring
Score each lead 0–5 for: Canada eligibility, verified funding, clarity of acceptance criteria, fit with AI-assisted implementation, expected review effort, time-to-payment, zero-cost feasibility, and risk. Record evidence and date checked. Do not treat advertised maximum rates as typical earnings.

## First service hypothesis
A read-only ecommerce listing preflight audit: review a customer-provided CSV, flag missing or inconsistent listing fields, estimate basic contribution where inputs permit, and provide a remediation checklist. No marketplace login, listing edits, publishing, or sales guarantee.

## Guardrails
Do not depend on crypto or speculative gains to fund this division. Never pay to access a job, unlock tasks, receive earnings, or pass a supposed qualification. Owner personally completes identity, bank, tax, and legal declarations. Obtain owner approval before public claims, client contact, bounty submissions, publication, spending, merges, or deployments. No income is guaranteed.
