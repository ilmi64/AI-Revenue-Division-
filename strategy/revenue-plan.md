# Revenue Plan

## Objective
Build a diversified, low-risk revenue pipeline using AI to reduce production effort while keeping human qualification, judgement, and approvals explicit.

## Priority order
1. **AI training/evaluation platforms:** potentially accessible without building a product first. Tasks are human work; AI can help prepare but must not impersonate the contributor.
2. **Funded software work:** pursue only issues with verifiable funding, clear acceptance criteria, and realistic skills fit.
3. **AI-assisted services:** start with a narrow, demonstrable deliverable such as a read-only ecommerce listing preflight audit.
4. **Reusable products:** package proven workflows only after demand is evidenced.
5. **Micro-tools:** build only when a specific user problem and a free operating path are validated.

## Opportunity scoring
Score each lead 0–5 for: Canada eligibility, evidence of current demand, payment clarity, fit with available skills, time-to-first-payment, zero-cost feasibility, and risk. Record evidence and date checked. Do not treat advertised maximum rates as typical earnings.

## First service hypothesis
A read-only ecommerce listing preflight audit: review a customer-provided CSV, flag missing or inconsistent listing fields, estimate basic contribution where inputs permit, and provide a remediation checklist. No marketplace login, listing edits, publishing, or sales guarantee.

## Guardrails
Do not depend on crypto or speculative gains to fund this division. Never pay to access a job, unlock tasks, receive earnings, or pass a supposed qualification. Stop and escalate if terms, identity requests, or payout conditions are unclear.
