# Zero-Cost Policy

Default approved spend is CAD $0.

- Prefer free tiers, public datasets, local tools, and existing authorised accounts.
- Check quotas, commercial-use terms, privacy terms, and payment requirements before relying on a free service.
- Do not enter a payment card or activate a paid trial without explicit approval.
- No paid APIs, domains, hosting, ads, subscriptions, credits, deposits, or outsourced labour by default.
- Free services can change limits or terms; record the date checked and a fallback.
- Do not upload confidential customer data to public AI services without appropriate permission and safeguards.
- If a workflow cannot operate safely at $0, pause and present the cost and alternatives instead of spending.
