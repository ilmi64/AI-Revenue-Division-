# Approval Policy

## AI may do independently
- Research public information and record dated sources.
- Draft internal plans, code, documentation, checklists, and reports.
- Run local or repository-safe tests and inspect public project files.
- Prepare application materials for owner review without submitting them.
- Estimate economics with assumptions clearly labelled.

## Owner approval required before
- Creating external accounts or changing account/security permissions.
- Submitting applications, assessments, identity documents, or personal attestations.
- Claiming or submitting a bounty, opening a client engagement, or contacting a prospect.
- Publishing products, posts, listings, public releases, or marketing claims.
- Spending money, starting paid trials, purchasing services, or making financial transactions.
- Merging pull requests, deploying software, changing production systems, or making irreversible changes.

## Human-only actions
The owner must personally perform identity verification, certify qualifications, complete tests that require their own unaided judgement, agree to platform/client contracts, and confirm tax or legal declarations.

## Approval record
For each consequential action record: requested action, scope, risks, cost, expected outcome, owner decision, and date. Silence is not approval.
