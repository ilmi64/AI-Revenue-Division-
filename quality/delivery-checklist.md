# Delivery Quality Checklist

- [ ] Scope and acceptance criteria are written.
- [ ] Inputs and assumptions are documented.
- [ ] Output is complete, readable, and reproducible.
- [ ] Tests include normal, boundary, missing, malformed, and adversarial inputs where relevant.
- [ ] Results have been independently checked where feasible.
- [ ] Estimates are labelled and not presented as actual results.
- [ ] Limitations and unsupported cases are disclosed.
- [ ] No secrets or unauthorised personal/customer data are included.
- [ ] Links and references are valid and dated.
- [ ] Owner approval obtained for any external delivery or publication.
