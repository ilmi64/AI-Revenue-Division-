# Profitability Model

Calculate per job or product using actual inputs wherever possible.

- Gross revenue = amount accepted/earned under the platform or client terms.
- Platform/payment fees = recorded fees and currency conversion costs.
- Direct costs = approved tools, hosting, materials, and other job-specific expenses.
- Net cash result = cash received minus fees and direct costs.
- Effective hourly return = net cash result divided by actual hours spent.

Keep separate fields for:
1. Estimate (unconfirmed).
2. Approved/accepted amount (not necessarily paid).
3. Cash received (verified in payout/account record).

Record CAD equivalent and exchange-rate source/date when paid in another currency. Include taxes only as a separate planning estimate; seek qualified Canadian tax advice for personal filing obligations. Do not count speculative token value or unpaid balances as cash revenue.
