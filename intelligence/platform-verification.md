## Verification checklist
- Use the official provider page, not an aggregator, as final evidence.
- Confirm the exact role/project is open to a Canadian resident in the user's province.
- Record task type, human effort, qualifications, expected volume, compensation basis, payment schedule, minimum payout, method, fees, and tax documentation.
- Check privacy, data handling, exclusivity, and whether AI assistance is allowed.
- Never pay to join, unlock tasks, receive earnings, or obtain a job.
- Do not submit applications or assessments until owner approval.
