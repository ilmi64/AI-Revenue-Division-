# AI Coding-to-Payment Playbook

## Primary model
Use AI to do the bulk of the engineering for clearly funded, bounded software tasks. The owner remains the legal account holder and must complete identity, banking, tax, contractual, and any human-only requirements.

## Preferred source: BOSS
Official site: https://www.boss.dev/
Documentation: https://www.boss.dev/doc/

BOSS documentation currently lists Canada as a supported country for earning bounties. It describes individual-person payouts to a bank account (international) and requires a GitHub account, BOSS account, personal details for financial regulations, and a qualifying issue closure. Re-check the current terms and account setup before relying on this.

Important: BOSS says bounty earnings are for individuals, not businesses, at present. Do not represent the organisation as the payee if the platform requires an individual. The owner must decide whether to participate personally and comply with applicable terms and tax obligations.

## Task selection filters
Only shortlist a task when all are true:
- Public, legitimate project and issue.
- Funding is visible and attached to the exact issue.
- Issue is open, unclaimed/unresolved, and bounty terms are current.
- Canada and payout eligibility are confirmed.
- Acceptance criteria are specific and testable.
- Scope is small enough to complete and review safely.
- No payment, deposit, paid membership, or purchase is required to participate.
- Required tools and dependencies can be used within the CAD $0 policy.
- No request for credential theft, malware, spam, exploit abuse, or unsafe access.

## AI execution workflow
1. Discover and verify candidate tasks; record source URL and date.
2. Read repository guidance, licence, tests, security policy, and issue discussion.
3. Estimate scope, likely review burden, acceptance risk, and effective hourly return.
4. Present a short decision brief to the owner before claiming or announcing work.
5. After approval, create a dedicated branch and implement the smallest complete fix.
6. Add or update tests; run available checks; inspect the diff for regressions and secrets.
7. Prepare a concise PR description with problem, solution, tests, limitations, and issue-closing reference.
8. Obtain owner approval before public claim, issue comment, or PR submission.
9. Monitor maintainer feedback; AI may prepare revisions, but owner approval remains required before consequential public changes.
10. Track accepted amount separately from payout and record actual receipt only when confirmed.

## Minimal owner interaction
One-time/setup actions may include BOSS account registration, connecting GitHub, identity and bank details, and tax information. The owner must do these personally. For each task, the target is one compact approval decision before external commitment and one approval before submission, with further involvement only if a maintainer asks questions or requests changes.

## No guarantees
A visible bounty is not guaranteed income. Maintainers control acceptance; duplicate solutions, changed requirements, incomplete work, or rejection may result in no payment. Never claim a bounty without reading its terms. Never share passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or account codes with the AI.
