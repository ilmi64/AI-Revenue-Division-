# Digital Product Workflow

1. Identify a repeated problem supported by evidence, not assumptions.
2. Define the target user and a small testable solution.
3. Check competing products, licences, platform fees, and free distribution options.
4. Build a minimum useful version using free tools only.
5. Test with synthetic examples and document known limitations.
6. Prepare a sample, instructions, support boundaries, and truthful product copy.
7. Ask the owner to approve public release, pricing, platform, and claims.
8. Track visits, conversion, refunds, support time, fees, and net receipts.
9. Improve only where evidence supports the effort.

Do not build a large product before validating demand. Do not use copyrighted assets or customer data without rights and permission.
