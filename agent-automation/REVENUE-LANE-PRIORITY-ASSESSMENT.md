**Purpose:** rank candidate revenue lanes by practical path to first legitimate payment, not advertised rates or theoretical upside.

## Evidence and operating notes

- **Contra:** payout options include local bank transfer, PayPal, Payoneer and crypto. Its published fee page lists Canada CAD EFT at USD $1 and CAD conversion at 0.40%; actual processor fees are shown at withdrawal. Sources: https://help.contra.com/en/articles/10008642-payouts and https://help.contra.com/en/articles/9322934-fees-overview
- **Outlier:** Canada is listed among supported locations, but individual projects can impose narrower location limits. The FAQ describes weekly Tuesday payouts by PayPal, AirTM or ACH and an associate-degree minimum. Sources: https://outlier.ai/legal/flexible-working-guidelines and https://outlier.ai/faq
- **Alignerr:** onboarding includes screening/identity verification and Stripe Connect or PayPal depending on location; do not assume every Canadian applicant or project qualifies. Source: https://www.alignerr.com/process
- **Upwork:** freelancer fees range from 0% to 15% per contract; local bank payout outside the US is listed at USD $0.99. Connects are required for many proposals and cost USD $0.15 each when purchased. Sources: https://www.upwork.com/pricing/freelancer and https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060918-How-to-get-paid-on-Upwork
- **Bugcrowd:** bank transfer and PayPal are supported; its documentation lists a CAD EFT minimum of $1 for Canadian merchant accounts. Sources: https://docs.bugcrowd.com/researchers/payments/setting-up-payment-methods/ and https://docs.bugcrowd.com/researchers/payments/setting-up-payment-methods/setting-up-bank-transfer-payment-method/
- **HackerOne:** Canadian-dollar bank transfers are listed, with a local transfer minimum of $50; PayPal and wallet payments are also described. Source: https://docs.hackerone.com/en/articles/8395720-payment-preferences
- **Gumroad:** no monthly charge; direct/profile sales are 10% + $0.50 plus processing, and Discover sales are 30%. Source: https://gumroad.com/pricing
- **NRC IRAP:** minimum criteria include Canadian incorporation, for-profit operation, up to 500 full-time-equivalent employees, and innovative technology commercialization. Source: https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2026/cnrc-nrc/NR16-208-2026-eng.pdf
- **Government of Canada Benefits Finder:** program eligibility varies; use it as a discovery directory, not a promise of funding. Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/finder.html

## Activation gates

1. Confirm the specific task or client is real, current, and within the user's demonstrated ability.
2. Verify Ontario/Canada eligibility for that exact role, program or project.
3. Confirm payout method, minimum, currency conversion, platform fees, KYC/tax requirements and withdrawal timing before work begins.
4. Check contract/IP ownership, dispute, refund and chargeback terms.
5. Do not pay for memberships, Connects, training, verification, deposits or equipment to unlock work without separate explicit approval.
6. Never test systems outside a program's written scope.
7. Record a lead as **opportunity**, not income. Record revenue only after work is earned under verified terms; record cash received separately.
8. No applications, proposals, outreach, submissions, publishing, account connections, purchases or financial transactions occur automatically.
