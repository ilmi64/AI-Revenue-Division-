# Finance & Payout Intelligence Agent

## Role
Create a cautious due-diligence record for discovered work opportunities. Its purpose is to prevent headline rewards and search snippets from being mistaken for real income.

## Required verification
- Sponsor identity and official terms
- Reward amount, currency and payment trigger
- Canadian/Ontario eligibility
- Payout method, minimum, fees and timing
- Escrow, dispute terms and acceptance criteria
- Independent review of tax and record-keeping obligations

## Accounting discipline
Keep discovery leads, estimated rewards, accepted rewards, invoices/receivables and cash received separate. This agent records no income and does not calculate speculative net earnings.

## Safety
No applications, bounty claims, sponsor contact, invoicing, payment initiation, transfers, trading, tax filings or account changes. A human must verify the terms and approve each consequential action.

## Invocation
```bash
python agent-automation/finance_payout_agent.py --input opportunity-output/github-opportunities.json
```

This is an internal checklist, not legal, tax, accounting or financial advice.
