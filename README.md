# AI Revenue Division

A zero-upfront-cost, AI-first operating unit for building diversified, legitimate revenue streams for the wider organisation.

## Mission
Identify real demand, validate opportunities before investing time, produce useful deliverables with AI assistance, and convert validated work into sustainable revenue. No income is guaranteed.

## Revenue channels
1. AI training and evaluation work (human qualification and judgement may be required).
2. Verified, funded software issues and bounties.
3. AI-assisted services (for example, ecommerce listing preflight audits and documentation).
4. Reusable digital products and templates.
5. Small AI tools only after demand and free-hosting feasibility are validated.

## Operating principles
- Default budget: CAD $0. No paid subscriptions, credits, ads, deposits, or infrastructure without explicit owner approval.
- AI may research, draft, code, test, document, compare, and prepare internal reports.
- The owner must personally complete identity checks, attestations, skill tests, human-evaluation tasks, and any work requiring their own judgement or legal agreement.
- No fabricated qualifications, portfolio claims, customer results, or earnings claims.
- No client contact, applications, bounty claims/submissions, public product launches, purchases, payments, merges, or deployments without the required owner approval.
- Keep credentials, private keys, tokens, customer secrets, and confidential data out of this public repository.
- Track estimates separately from approved earnings and money actually received.

## Repository map
- `strategy/`: strategy, approvals, and zero-cost rules.
- `intelligence/`: platform verification and opportunity tracker.
- `operations/`: bounty, service, and product workflows.
- `quality/`: delivery and security checks.
- `finance/`: profitability model and revenue ledger.
- `reports/`: weekly operating report template.

## Status vocabulary
`research`, `verified`, `applied`, `qualified`, `in_progress`, `submitted`, `accepted`, `approved_unpaid`, `paid`, `rejected`, `closed`.

## Current status
Foundation repository established. Opportunities must be re-verified at the time of application because eligibility, task supply, rates, and payment terms change.
