# Animora Studio Funding Framework

**Status:** Costing framework only. No funding allocation or purchase is authorised.  
**Currency:** CAD unless a line item explicitly states otherwise.

## Funding principles

- The organisation's current no-upfront-spend rule remains in force.
- Do not count forecasts, token balances, unsettled receivables or unverified platform balances as available funding.
- Separate revenue earned from cash received; only reconciled, available cash can fund commitments.
- Protect essential operating reserves, taxes, refunds, liabilities and existing approved commitments before assigning studio funds.
- Every proposal needs a defined scope, owner, total cost, recurring cost, exit cost, downside case and measurable acceptance criteria.
- Do not start billing-risk trials or activate paid services to obtain a quote or test a workflow.

## Funding gate calculation

**Available studio funding** should be calculated only after reconciliation:

`Available funding = verified unrestricted CAD cash received - essential reserves - taxes/liabilities - approved commitments`

The owner may allocate a portion of available funding to an Animora reserve. The reserve is not a spending authority: each commitment still needs its own approval.

## Scenario analysis

Prepare low, base and high scenarios for:
- A lean development and proof-of-concept stage.
- A small repeatable production pipeline.
- A first funded studio release.
- The integrated store setup and first approved product collection.
- Ongoing monthly operations and a delayed-revenue scenario.

For each scenario record assumptions, evidence quality, cash timing, minimum runway, break-even assumptions and what gets paused first if revenue is delayed. Do not assume merchandise revenue will finance production before customer demand and unit economics are evidenced.

## Store opening gate

The future integrated Animora store should not open merely because a website can be published. It requires:
1. A funded Animora studio work package and accountable owners.
2. Cleared IP and product rights for every launch item.
3. Verified supplier samples, quality and delivery capability.
4. Destination-specific landed-cost and contribution calculations.
5. Approved budget and bounded inventory/fulfilment exposure.
6. Tax, privacy, consumer terms, shipping, returns and customer support review.
7. Tested order, payment, fulfilment, refund and support workflows.
8. Explicit owner approval for launch.

No verified studio funding pool, cost quotes, approved production budget, dedicated team or store-launch budget is recorded here. The framework is preparatory and does not alter the organisation's CAD 0 spend ceiling.
