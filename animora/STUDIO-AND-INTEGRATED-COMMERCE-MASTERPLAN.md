# Animora Studio and Integrated Commerce Masterplan

**Status:** Strategic build plan; not a launch authorization  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-16  
**Funding rule:** No paid subscriptions, API usage, credits, trials with billing risk, production infrastructure, hiring, inventory, advertising or other spending until the organisation is generating revenue and the owner explicitly approves the specific commitment.

## 1. Strategic direction

Animora is intended to grow from its current anime-discovery application into an original creative, media and entertainment studio. The current website is a foundation and audience touchpoint, not the full business.

The future studio may develop and own original intellectual property (IP), characters, worlds, stories and productions, then commercialise that IP through entertainment, publishing, licensing, merchandise and other approved channels.

The current standalone ecommerce operation is **not** assumed to be the permanent store or brand. The likely direction is to retire or substantially restructure the standalone storefront and, when Animora is properly funded and ready, launch commerce as an integrated part of the Animora ecosystem. This is a strategic direction, not an instruction to delete existing accounts, listings, assets or records.

## 2. Animora business pillars

1. **Digital platform and audience:** anime discovery, character experiences, community features and a reliable audience relationship.
2. **Original IP and development:** character bibles, world bibles, story development, visual identity, rights records and development pipelines. Existing characters Lyra, Kaito, Miko and Solen are continuity assets to preserve.
3. **Creative production:** animation, short-form films, proof-of-concept sequences, trailers, visual effects, CGI and eventually longer-form productions, subject to financing and production capacity.
4. **Publishing and creative products:** manga, comics, artbooks, soundtracks and digital creative releases where rights and production quality are established.
5. **Commercial and licensing:** merchandise, collaborations, licensing, distribution, commissioned work and partnerships under written approval and rights controls.
6. **Integrated commerce:** a future Animora-owned shop experience serving the studio's IP and audience rather than an unrelated standalone catalogue.

These are strategic capability areas, not claims that production teams, rights clearance, distribution deals or revenue currently exist.

## 3. Future store scope

The integrated store may eventually include:
- Apparel and accessories, including clothing collections tied to original IP.
- Character merchandise and approved character designs.
- Collectibles, figures, plush, posters, prints and limited editions.
- Products inspired by Animora films, series, stories and creative releases.
- Publishing: manga, comics, artbooks and behind-the-scenes books.
- Digital products such as wallpapers, art packs and licensed digital content.
- Collaborations, seasonal releases and exclusive drops.
- Carefully controlled third-party licensing and distribution.

Every product must pass IP ownership/licensing, quality, supplier, landed-cost, tax, fulfilment, returns, customer-service, safety and contribution-margin checks. No product is automatically approved because it fits the theme.

## 4. Staged build and funding gates

### Stage A — Foundation (current, zero-spend)
- Preserve and audit the existing Animora application, original characters, art assets and technical decisions.
- Document the studio vision, IP catalogue, creative development pipeline and product principles.
- Map required capabilities, roles, equipment, software, infrastructure, legal/IP work and indicative costs without activating paid services.
- Improve only what can be safely researched or completed using existing free access and without consuming restricted Lovable credits.
- Keep the current store and its records available for audit; do not commit to a new brand, delete assets or publish new products.
- Establish baseline audience, product and operating metrics; small analytics samples must not be treated as proof of product-market fit.

**Exit evidence:** approved studio brief, preserved asset/IP inventory, technical and privacy gap list, phased budget estimate, risk register and owner-approved scope for the next stage.

### Stage B — Revenue and capital formation
- Build organisation-wide revenue first; Animora does not receive unfunded commitments.
- Maintain separate records for revenue earned, cash actually received, committed costs and restricted funds.
- Propose a dedicated Animora reserve only after cash receipts are evidenced and essential operating obligations and reserves are covered.
- Set a written funding envelope for each proposed Animora work package.

**Exit evidence:** reconciled cash receipts, positive available funding after reserves, approved budget, costed work packages and a documented runway. Revenue forecasts alone do not satisfy this gate.

### Stage C — Funded studio formation
- Approve the first production scope and appoint accountable creative, technical, production and finance owners.
- Select tools, contractors, equipment and infrastructure only after comparison of total cost, rights, security, Canadian availability and exit options.
- Establish IP chain-of-title, contributor agreements, releases, licensing, privacy, safeguarding and production recordkeeping with qualified legal advice where required.
- Build a repeatable production pipeline and quality-control process before scaling output.

**Exit evidence:** funded and approved production plan, rights clearance, assigned owners, tested workflow, budget controls, delivery acceptance criteria and contingency plan.

### Stage D — Integrated store readiness and launch
The proper store launch is tied to the funded Animora studio milestone. It is not an early standalone ecommerce launch.

Before opening:
- Storefront and fulfilment architecture are approved and tested.
- Products are tied to owned or properly licensed Animora IP.
- Supplier samples and quality standards are accepted.
- Full landed economics are verified for target destinations, including platform/payment fees, shipping, tax treatment, returns and customer support.
- Inventory or print-on-demand exposure is bounded by an approved budget.
- Product pages, accessibility, privacy, consumer terms, shipping/returns and support processes are reviewed.
- Demand evidence and production capacity support the launch scope.
- Owner explicitly approves the launch and each consequential external action.

**Exit evidence:** completed launch checklist, signed-off product economics, tested fulfilment and returns, rights register, risk approval, support coverage and owner launch approval.

### Stage E — Scale the creative ecosystem
Expand production formats, catalogue, licensing, distribution, collaborations and international commerce only when each expansion has its own evidence, rights clearance, funding and capacity gate.

## 5. Decision rights and safeguards

- ChatGPT may research, draft, compare, inspect and prepare proposals; it does not independently commit funds or make binding business decisions.
- No paid service, trial with billing risk, API plan, software upgrade, Lovable credit use, hiring, purchase, inventory order, application, outreach, publishing, contract, account change or deployment without explicit owner approval.
- Do not delete, close or migrate the current ecommerce store or its listings without an approved transition and records-retention plan.
- Do not assume the MidnightOtaku identity will carry forward. Preserve its assets and performance data for later evaluation.
- Animora must maintain clear IP provenance. Do not use third-party anime characters, protected artwork, music, logos or trademarks without appropriate rights.
- Avoid claims of guaranteed commercial success, audience demand or profitability.
- All financial forecasts must distinguish estimates from verified cash, and include downside scenarios.
- Canada/Ontario requirements, privacy, consumer protection, tax and IP obligations must be reviewed before commercial launch.
- Any faith-sensitive financial or commercial questions should be flagged for qualified review rather than treated as an automated ruling.

## 7. Current baseline and limitations

The 2026-11-16 read-only audit records the current Lovable site and its known gaps in `animora/READINESS-AUDIT-2026-11-16.md`. The current site is not evidence of a production studio, persistent AI chat, mature community, commercial demand or a funded production pipeline. Lovable credits remain restricted. No new app edits or deployment are authorized by this plan.

The existing ecommerce calculator is a preparation tool only. It does not establish actual SKU profitability, demand, supplier performance or launch readiness. The current store's future status remains a decision for a later evidence-based review.
