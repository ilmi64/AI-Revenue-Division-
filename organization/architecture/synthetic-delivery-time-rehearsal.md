## Evidence labels
- SYNTHETIC FACT: supplied by the fictional scenario.
- CALCULATION: reproducible arithmetic from identified inputs.
- ASSUMPTION: selected but unverified input.
- HYPOTHESIS: possible explanation or improvement requiring validation.
- UNKNOWN: unavailable or unchecked information.

Never turn assumptions into findings. Every calculation must show inputs, formula, unit, period and sensitivity. If no baseline exists, state that impact cannot be quantified.

## Quality acceptance
- [ ] One workflow only; scope and exclusions are explicit.
- [ ] All data are synthetic and labelled.
- [ ] Map identifies trigger, owner, steps, handoffs, systems and end state.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns are distinguished.
- [ ] No invented savings, conversion uplift or revenue claims.
- [ ] Calculations are reproducible and unit-consistent.
- [ ] Recommendations state impact, effort, dependency, risk and cost.
- [ ] Free/manual options are separated from paid dependencies.
- [ ] No credentials, sensitive personal data or real customer records.
- [ ] Privacy, security, retention and access limitations are explicit.
- [ ] Report is understandable and independently traceable.
- [ ] Revisions and rework minutes are recorded.

## Economics capture
Record total labour including preparation, QA, administration and rework; management-selected shadow labour rate; direct tool/inference/hosting cost; revisions/support allowance; and total economic cost/contribution at hypothetical price points. Do not invent a labour rate or set price from competitor advertisements alone. Use the separate mini-review economics model as the controlling worksheet.
