# Practical Operations Improvement Audit

**Internal draft — not published, not quoted, not offered to customers.**

## Make one frustrating business workflow easier to run
Small businesses can lose time when enquiries, quotes, bookings, follow-ups or routine administration are spread across disconnected steps and tools.

The proposed Practical Operations Improvement Audit reviews one workflow, shows how it operates today, identifies friction and prioritizes practical improvements.

## Proposed deliverables
- A structured review of one agreed workflow.
- A current-state process map showing steps, handoffs and responsibilities.
- Up to three prioritized improvement opportunities.
- A conservative time-and-cost model when reliable baseline information exists.
- An implementation roadmap with dependencies, effort and risks.
- Privacy, security, data-minimization and cost considerations.
- A no-cost or existing-tool option where feasible.

## Exclusions
- No guaranteed savings, revenue growth, lead volume or automation accuracy.
- No installation, deployment, production integration or data migration.
- No access to inboxes, payment systems, customer databases or private accounts.
- No legal, tax, accounting or regulated professional advice.
- No ongoing support or unlimited revisions.
- No undisclosed paid software dependency.

## Proposed process
1. Agree on one workflow and its boundaries.
2. Review information supplied by the business, subject to approved privacy and data-handling terms.
3. Map the process and distinguish evidence from assumptions.
4. Assess improvements, effort, cost, risks and measurable outcomes.
5. Deliver a concise report and acceptance checklist.

## Potential fit
Owner-operated and small service businesses with recurring enquiries, quoting, bookings, follow-up, document handling or repetitive administration. The review is unsuitable where the problem, baseline, authority to share process details or expected benefit cannot be established.

## Commercial status
This is an internal offer concept. Scope, delivery time, price, terms, buyer demand and delivery economics have not been validated. This draft authorizes no customer outreach or public distribution.

**Positioning principle:** practical business outcomes first; AI is only a production method where it improves quality, cost or speed.
