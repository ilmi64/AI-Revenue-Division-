# AI Revenue Company — Operating Blueprint

**Status:** Architecture and operating design; not evidence of staffed or live operations  
**Company:** AI Revenue Company  
**Authority:** AI Revenue CEO and independent company executive authority  
**Build mode:** Parallel with Trading & Technology; independent launch gate  
**Budget:** CAD $0 incremental by default; all spending and paid services remain approval-gated

## 1. Company mandate

AI Revenue is a complete AI-native operating company. It is not a Trading & Technology support department, a shared-services pool, or a company whose launch depends on trading readiness or revenue from another business.

It identifies commercial problems, creates useful AI-enabled products/services, acquires customers, delivers accepted work, supports customers, manages its own finances and improves its operations. It owns its leadership, policies, agent workforce, data, memory, queues, audit records, budget and launch decision.

## 2. Executive operating system

| Executive role | Accountability | Reserved boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | Strategy, company priorities, executive decisions, launch recommendation | Cannot override owner approval requirements |
| COO | Operating cadence, cross-department delivery, quality and service reliability | Cannot spend or make external commitments without authority |
| CFO | Budget, pricing economics, cash, accounting, invoices and financial controls | No unauthorized transfers, payments or paid services |
| CTO | Engineering, architecture, model/runtime integration, product reliability | No unapproved installs, deployments, paid APIs or permission changes |
| CMO | Brand, positioning, content, SEO, distribution and campaign learning | No unapproved publishing or ad spend |
| CRO | Offer design, pipeline, prospect qualification, proposals and sales process | No unapproved customer contact, contracts or commitments |
| CHRO / Workforce lead | Agent roles, task allocation, evaluation, workload and handovers | Cannot grant agents broader authority than policy allows |
| CISO | Access control, privacy, security, backup, incident and recovery | Cannot bypass owner approval or company policy |
| Legal/compliance review | Issue spotting for contracts, IP, privacy, consumer/platform rules and jurisdiction | Escalates qualified legal matters; not a substitute for counsel |

Roles may initially be performed by a compact workforce, but each responsibility must have an explicitly named accountable role and review path.

## 3. Department operating map

### A. Product & Service Development
- Intake customer problems and internal product hypotheses.
- Research demand, competition, willingness-to-pay signals and delivery feasibility.
- Write product/service briefs with buyer, problem, outcome, scope, exclusions, price logic and acceptance criteria.
- Prioritize a narrow initial offer portfolio; do not build speculative products without evidence.
- Maintain roadmap, change log, customer feedback and sunset decisions.

### B. Engineering & Automation
- Build websites, workflow automation, integrations, internal tools and product components.
- Maintain version control, documentation, test plans, secrets boundaries and rollback/recovery plans.
- Separate development, review and release responsibilities.
- Keep integrations read-only until their permissions and external-action scope are approved.
- No deployments, account permission changes or paid API use without explicit approval.

### C. AI Creative Studio
- Creative briefs, scripts, storyboards, brand systems, promotional videos, ad creative, social assets, product imagery and editing workflows.
- HyperFrames and ComfyUI remain priority candidates for research/build preparation.
- Maintain rights/provenance checks, client approval, brand consistency, export quality and revision tracking.
- Creative delivery does not authorize public publishing; the customer/owner approves release.
- No paid credits or subscriptions without approval.

### D. Sales & Business Development
- Define ideal customer profiles and qualification criteria.
- Build a prospect research pipeline using lawful public information.
- Draft offers, proposals, scopes, pricing and statements of work.
- Track lead stage, next action, probability and evidence.
- External messages, calls, proposals and contracts require the appropriate owner approval.

### E. Marketing & Distribution
- Positioning, SEO, educational content, social content calendars, landing-page copy and campaign analysis.
- Use organic/free channels first; TikTok excluded unless reintroduced.
- Maintain platform-specific approval before posting.
- Track source, reach, qualified leads and conversion without claiming attribution unsupported by evidence.

### F. Customer Success & Support
- Approved onboarding, requirements capture, status updates, support triage, feedback and retention.
- Maintain customer-specific access boundaries and minimal necessary data.
- Define response expectations and escalation.
- No customer-facing action until authorized and the customer relationship is established.

### G. Delivery & Quality Assurance
- Convert approved scope into tasks, milestones and acceptance criteria.
- Conduct independent QA, accessibility/usability checks where relevant, security review and client handover.
- Record completion evidence, revisions, acceptance and unresolved limitations.
- Do not claim a deliverable is complete until required acceptance evidence exists.

### H. Business & Product Intelligence
- Coordinate with Research & Intelligence through scoped briefs.
- Analyze demand, customer pain, competitor offers, price ranges, platform changes and distribution opportunities.
- Maintain evidence grades and distinguish public claims from verified demand.
- Company-private customer data remains inside AI Revenue.

### I. Finance & Administration
- Independent ledger, budget, cash forecast, costs, pricing, invoices, receivables and profitability.
- Track unpaid invoices and payment evidence; distinguish booked, invoiced, received and cleared funds.
- No financial transaction, payment, transfer, subscription or purchase without explicit approval.
- Include Canadian/Ontario tax, privacy and consumer obligations for qualified review; do not invent legal conclusions.

### J. Security, Privacy & Recovery
- Company-specific identity and least-privilege access.
- Secret management outside model context; no credentials in prompts or shared research.
- Data minimization, retention, backups, restore procedures and incident response.
- Customer and company records cannot flow to Trading & Technology by default.
- Test company-local operation if shared infrastructure or Main Hub is unavailable.

### K. Agent Workforce Operations
- Maintain company-specific agent directory, role cards, capability register, task queue, memory scope, policy profile and audit identity.
- Define human/owner review points, deterministic policy enforcement, escalation and task handoff.
- Models can draft, analyze and recommend; they cannot grant themselves permissions or authorize external actions.
- Agent evaluations include task quality, groundedness, privacy compliance, cost, reliability and escalation behaviour.

## 4. Initial commercial delivery system

The revenue-first, zero-upfront sequence, offer ranking, first workflow-audit specification, commercial funnel and approval controls are maintained in the [Revenue-First, Zero-Upfront Commercialization Plan](revenue-first-commercialization-plan.md). This plan is the current preparation guide; it does not claim validated demand or authorize customer contact.


Every offer must have:
1. A defined buyer and validated problem.
2. A clear outcome and scope, exclusions and assumptions.
3. A delivery method and estimated labour/compute requirements.
4. Price logic and cost ceiling.
5. Acceptance criteria and QA checklist.
6. Customer data and privacy handling.
7. Support, revision and handover rules.
8. An approval path for proposals, customer communication, contracts and publication.

Candidate offer families to evaluate—not claims of current availability or sales:
- AI workflow and business-process automation.
- Small-business websites and landing pages.
- Content/creative production packages.
- SEO/content operations and research briefs.
- Digital templates, guides and workflow assets.
- Liquidation inventory intelligence and listing operations for resellers/liquidators: manifest normalization, SKU research, conservative resale economics, listing preparation and inventory workflow automation. See [Liquidation Inventory & Resale Business Model Assessment](research/liquidation-inventory-resale-business-model-assessment.md). This is a capital-light service hypothesis; inventory-owning pallet resale remains separately gated on approved capital, verified supply, inspection, compliance and positive conservative unit economics.
- Later: proprietary tools and SaaS only after demand and maintenance economics are demonstrated.

Start with the narrowest deliverable that can be completed reliably at zero or negligible incremental cost. Do not promise autonomous services before the underlying process has been tested.

## 5. Standard customer lifecycle

1. Opportunity intake and lawful prospect research.
2. Qualification and fit assessment.
3. Draft scope, price and proposal.
4. Owner-approved customer contact and commitment.
5. Requirements and data intake with minimization.
6. Delivery plan, milestones and internal QA.
7. Customer review and authorized revisions.
8. Acceptance, handover and support.
9. Invoice/payment tracking and financial reconciliation.
10. Feedback, retention and product learning.

The workflow is a target design. No customer acquisition, contact, contract, publication, invoice submission or payment action is implied by this document.

## 6. AI workforce / swarm design

- AI Revenue has its own company identity namespace and isolated workforce tenant.
- CEO/COO agents coordinate internal tasks within company policy.
- Department agents operate with role-specific scopes and least privilege.
- Specialist sub-agents may research, draft, code, review, test or analyze; external actions remain separately gated.
- A critic/reviewer role challenges material outputs; high-impact work receives independent review.
- Task messages include requester, owner, purpose, allowed data, allowed tools, deadline, expected artifact, evidence requirements and escalation path.
- Memory is separated into company policy, department knowledge, project/customer scope and task-local context.
- Shared orchestration/model adapters may be reused, but company-specific prompts, memory, task queues, identities, permissions and audit streams remain separate.
- A failed shared agent platform must not prevent the company from using a bounded manual/local fallback.

## 7. Independent launch-readiness gates

| Gate | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Governance | CEO/authority map, reserved approvals and decision log |
| Workforce | Department responsibilities, agent identities, permissions, evaluation and escalation |
| Offer | At least one narrow offer fully specified with scope, economics, delivery and acceptance criteria |
| Delivery | Repeatable workflow, QA, revision and handover procedure |
| Customer operations | Onboarding, privacy, support and complaint/escalation process |
| Finance | Budget, cost ceiling, pricing, invoice/payment tracking and cash controls |
| Security | Least privilege, secret handling, data retention, backup and recovery |
| Technology | Reproducible build/release process and rollback; external deployment separately approved |
| Operational rehearsal | Tabletop or controlled rehearsal of failed delivery, data incident, customer escalation and service outage |
| Launch decision | Independent AI Revenue executive decision based on evidence |

Commercial traction and actual payment are important business outcomes and should be tracked honestly. They are not prerequisites imposed on Trading & Technology, and Trading & Technology readiness is not a prerequisite for AI Revenue.

## 8. Parallel operation and independence

- Build this company in parallel with Trading & Technology.
- Do not use Trading & Technology capital, customer records, executives, private research or risk authority as an assumed dependency.
- Do not provide Trading & Technology with AI Revenue customer data, internal product roadmaps or private company memory by default.
- Use approved shared infrastructure and scoped intelligence only.
- The company must retain a safe local operating mode if Trading & Technology, Main Hub/OCC, Research & Intelligence or shared orchestration is unavailable.
- Launch when its own gates are met; another lane's status cannot veto it.

## 9. Current restrictions and status

This blueprint is not evidence of a live AI company, deployed agents, active customer pipeline, accepted delivery or revenue. All such claims require direct evidence.

No paid subscriptions, API usage, credits, hosting, installations, trials with billing risk, publishing, customer contact, external commitments, financial transactions, deployments or permission changes are authorized by this blueprint. Existing free/current tools and documentation work only, within the standing approval rules.
