# AI Revenue — Practical Operations Improvement Mini-Review Offer Specification

**Status:** Internal offer design; not published, priced, sold or validated.
**Company:** AI Revenue Company only. **Budget:** CAD $0 incremental by default.
**Authority:** No prospect contact, public promotion, quote, contract, customer-data intake or paid delivery without separate explicit owner approval.

## 1. Offer
A focused, evidence-led review of one recurring business workflow showing how the process works, where friction or risk appears, and which three improvements should be considered first.

Sell the operational decision the buyer receives—not “AI transformation,” an autonomous agent or guaranteed savings.

## 2. Best-fit buyer hypothesis
A small Canadian service business where one repeated workflow creates delay, duplicate work, rework or lost follow-up; an owner/manager can explain and approve it; non-sensitive baseline information exists; and diagnosis can be done without production access, credentials or sensitive personal information.

Possible workflows: enquiry intake, quote follow-up, appointment preparation, document routing, internal status updates and repetitive reporting. These are examples, not validated verticals.

## 3. Fixed scope
One workflow / one business unit / one decision-maker / one defined process boundary.

Deliver:
1. Engagement and workflow boundary statement.
2. Current-state map of steps, roles, tools, handoffs and wait states.
3. Evidence table separating observed facts, client statements, calculations, assumptions and unknowns.
4. Baseline snapshot using safe, client-supplied aggregate information.
5. Friction and risk register.
6. Three prioritized improvements ranked by impact, effort, cost/dependency and risk.
7. Conservative impact model only where inputs support it; otherwise specify what must be measured.
8. Practical next-step plan with acceptance criteria and an existing-tool/no-cost option where feasible.
9. Privacy, security and data-minimization note.
10. Concise handover and independent QA review.

## 4. Exclusions
No implementation, integration, deployment, hosting, production changes, inbox/customer/payment-account access, credentials, private datasets, sensitive personal/financial/health data, customer messaging, legal/tax/accounting/cybersecurity certification, regulated advice, guaranteed ROI/savings/revenue/leads, unlimited revisions or ongoing support. Paid software/API/hosting dependencies require separate scope and approval.

Decline or defer regulated, safety-critical, sensitive, unclear-ownership workflows or any review requiring production access.

## 5. Engagement flow
1. Qualification only; no free custom audit or roadmap.
2. Confirm boundaries, evidence, exclusions and data handling.
3. Obtain owner approval and review contract/privacy/tax/payment terms.
4. Intake minimum approved, aggregate or redacted evidence.
5. Map the process and record friction.
6. Prioritize three improvements and calculate only what inputs support.
7. Independent QA of arithmetic, evidence, scope, clarity and privacy.
8. Handover, feedback, agreed data return/deletion and closeout.

## 6. Delivery measurement
The existing 180-minute target is an unvalidated ceiling, not a service promise. Measure the full cycle: acquisition, qualification, proposal, setup, preparation, analysis, report, QA, revisions, handover, administration, invoicing, closeout, rework and included support.

Record hands-on minutes separately from elapsed waiting time. Use the existing Full-Cycle Delivery Economics Capture Worksheet. No actual delivery-time, margin, price or customer-demand evidence exists.

## 7. Pricing gates
Do not set or publish a fee until at least two distinct authorized synthetic rehearsals measure full-cycle time and QA; direct costs and allocated labour are included; owner-approved shadow rate and economic floor are explicit; base/stress scenarios pass; tax, payment, refund, privacy, contract and retention responsibilities are reviewed; and separately approved validation produces genuine buyer feedback.

Competitor advertised ranges are context, not our price. Collected fees excluding applicable sales tax are revenue; pipeline, verbal interest and unpaid invoices are not.

## 8. Qualification checklist
- One clearly bounded workflow?
- Frequent or consequential enough to matter?
- Decision-maker can explain and own it?
- Safe, non-sensitive baseline evidence?
- Can review be done without account access or production changes?
- Buyer understands diagnosis, not implementation?
- Is there a real decision the report will support?

Hard stops: credential sharing, sensitive data, guaranteed outcomes, prohibited activity, unclear authority, unpaid custom consulting, unapproved spending or unsafe automation.
