# Parallel Build & Independent Launch Programme

**Status:** Canonical execution plan; planning and architecture only  
**Planning date:** 2026-11-21  
**Target:** Simultaneous parallel development with a coordinated January 4–8, 2027 launch target for both companies, subject to each company's independent readiness gates and Trading's lawful starting-capital requirement. If one company misses its own gates, the ready company may launch independently and the other remains on standby; no gate is waived and neither company is a prerequisite for the other.  
**Budget posture:** CAD $0 incremental by default; no paid services or spending without explicit owner approval  
**Applies to:** AI Revenue Company, Trading & Technology Company, Main Hub/OCC, and Research & Intelligence Division

## 1. Governing rule

Build the organization in parallel. AI Revenue and Trading & Technology are separate, standalone companies with separate leadership, agent workforces/swarms, task queues, memory, data, finances, policies, risk authorities and launch decisions.

They may share selected infrastructure solely to cooperate, reduce duplicated engineering and gain a competitive edge. Sharing infrastructure does not create shared company authority, finance, private data, customer records, trading records, executive control or a dependency on the other company.

Neither company is a prerequisite for the other. Neither company must fund, serve, supply, or reach revenue/operational status before the other can launch. A delay or failure in one lane must not block the other.

Main Hub/OCC is a coordination and evidence layer, not a parent operator or mandatory runtime dependency. Research & Intelligence is an organization-level division/shared service, not a third company. Neither Main Hub nor Research & Intelligence readiness is a prerequisite for either company launch.

## 2. Parallel work lanes

**Leadership and governance**

- Select a small number of no-cost-to-start offers with clear buyer, problem, scope, deliverables, price logic, acceptance criteria and delivery process.

### Lane C — Main Hub / OCC shared coordination layer

- Company adapters remain separate; approval for one company does not authorize the other.
- OCC outage must not stop either company's local operations.
- OCC can report and coordinate; it cannot execute company actions, control risk, move funds or approve launch.

## 3. Shared infrastructure workstream — common components, separate tenants

Potential common foundations include Paperclip (workforce management candidate), Ruflo (bounded orchestration candidate), model adapters, evaluation, tracing/observability and reusable engineering components.

For every shared component:
- define the component owner and maintenance boundary;
- define separate company tenants/configurations and service identities;
- keep agent IDs, memory, prompts/context, task queues, credentials and audit streams separate;
- expose only documented, minimum-scope interfaces;
- prevent one company from reading or changing the other's records;
- ensure a shared component outage has a company-local fallback;
- test cross-tenant denial and independent recovery;
- verify licensing, model compatibility, data handling, compute and cost before adoption.

A shared platform is a convenience and advantage, not a central authority. If a shared service is unavailable, both companies must retain a safe standalone operating mode.

## 4. Coordination cadence and dependency rules

- Maintain four parallel work queues: AI Revenue, Trading & Technology, Main Hub/OCC and Research & Intelligence.
- Maintain separate company owners and acceptance criteria.
- Hold a coordination review to identify reusable components and intelligence exchanges, not to combine company authority.
- Cross-lane dependencies must be classified as **blocking**, **optional shared enhancement**, or **information-only**. Shared infrastructure and Research Division deliverables are optional enhancements unless a company independently elects to rely on a specific service.
- Every optional shared component needs a fallback plan before a company relies on it.
- No company may silently become the other's customer, supplier, capital source or operational support function.
- Research requests are scoped to the receiving company; private source material is not shared across companies.
- Preserve decisions and changes with versioned records; new decisions supersede old ones explicitly.

### Stage 4 — Coordinated launch window
- Build both companies simultaneously and pursue parallel launch if Trading capital is available and both companies independently pass their own readiness gates.
- If Trading capital is unavailable at the launch review, AI Revenue launches first only if its own readiness gate is passed and launch is separately authorized; Trading & Technology remains on standby for funded operations until capital and its own gates are ready.
- AI Revenue revenue may be considered as a possible future source of Trading capital only through a separately approved, legally and accountingly sound arrangement. No automatic intercompany transfer, loan, shared treasury, guarantee or funding obligation exists.
- Do not count projected, unclaimed, airdropped or hypothetical rewards as available capital. Do not borrow, use leverage, paid memberships or deposits to force launch.
- The coordinated January 4–8, 2027 window is a target, not a hold point. A ready company is not delayed solely because the other company is delayed.
- Never describe a company as launched, live or revenue-generating without verified evidence.
- Trading live execution remains a separate evidence-based readiness gate and requires explicit owner approval after company launch.

## 10. Owner's latest capital-contingency and launch-priority decision (2026-11-21)

This section is the latest controlling decision and supersedes earlier conflicting launch-sequencing statements in this programme, including any wording that makes AI Revenue's verified revenue an unconditional prerequisite for Trading & Technology launch or, conversely, assumes Trading must launch regardless of capital availability.

### Default operating plan — build both simultaneously and pursue parallel launch

- Continue building AI Revenue and Trading & Technology simultaneously, with separate company work queues, executive authority, finances, policies, records, risk controls and readiness gates.
- Pursue capital for Trading & Technology independently while building it. Do not pause Trading research, PolyBot, data, risk, security, paper-mode or operational-readiness work while capital is being pursued.
- If the required capital is available and Trading independently passes its company-launch gates, pursue launch alongside AI Revenue in the coordinated launch window. Do not delay a ready company solely because the other company is delayed.
- AI Revenue must independently establish a viable offer, delivery, customer, finance, security and support operation. Trading must independently establish its own legal, financial, data, risk, security, strategy and operational readiness.
- Company launch is not equivalent to live trading. Live execution remains separately disabled until its own evidence-based gate and explicit owner approval are satisfied.

### Capital-shortfall contingency — AI Revenue launches first; Trading stands by

- If Trading capital is not available when launch is considered, AI Revenue becomes the first company launch priority, provided AI Revenue itself passes its readiness gate and the owner authorizes launch.
- Trading & Technology remains in standby for funded/live operations rather than being represented as launched or live. Continue only the safe, authorized no-cost preparation and paper/research work that remains feasible.
- Reassess Trading when a legitimate capital source becomes available and its own gates are satisfied. Capital may be independently obtained; AI Revenue revenue may be considered as a possible future source only through a separately approved, legally/accountingly sound arrangement.
- There is no automatic transfer, loan, intercompany funding, shared treasury, cross-company guarantee or obligation for AI Revenue to fund Trading. Each company's finance and governance remain separate.
- If capital is not available, do not manufacture a launch through an airdrop, borrowed funds, paid memberships, leverage, deposits or speculative rewards. Wait rather than compromise capital preservation or risk controls.

### Decision rule and reporting

- **Now:** simultaneous build; parallel capital-readiness work; no company is put on hold merely because the other is still preparing.
- **At launch review:** if Trading capital and all Trading gates are ready, both companies may launch in parallel after their respective approvals. If Trading capital is unavailable, launch AI Revenue first if ready and authorized; mark Trading STANDBY / NOT LAUNCHED.
- **After AI Revenue launch:** continue AI Revenue commercial operations and Trading's independent preparation; revisit the Trading capital gate when evidence changes.
- Report actual capital availability, source, restrictions and approval separately from projected capital. Never call expected, airdropped, unclaimed or hypothetical funds available capital.
- This contingency changes sequencing only. It does not merge the companies, make AI Revenue subordinate to Trading, grant Main Hub/OCC authority, or authorize external financial actions.
