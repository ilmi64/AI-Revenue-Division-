## 2. Business question and limits

The exercises are intended to test whether the proposed Practical Operations Improvement Mini-Review can be delivered with:
- a consistent one-workflow boundary;
- traceable evidence and explicit assumptions/unknowns;
- reproducible calculations;
- a useful prioritised improvement plan;
- acceptable independent QA;
- measured active effort, elapsed time, rework and cost exposure.

They cannot establish buyer demand, willingness to pay, commercial conversion, customer delivery time, legal compliance, profitability, or market fit. The 210-minute rehearsal target is a measurement hypothesis, not a customer promise. The offer specification's 180-minute target is likewise unvalidated; the controlling rehearsal plan and actual time log must make any difference explicit rather than silently changing the target.

- Use only the exact committed fictional scenario packs and internal templates listed in Section 4.

## 6. Execution method — only after separate authorization

For each scenario:

1. Create a unique exercise ID and record authorization evidence before work begins.
2. Record exact source pack/template SHAs and confirm every input is fictional.
3. Start a contemporaneous activity log. Capture start/end timestamps, active minutes, elapsed minutes, role, activity and evidence reference. Do not reconstruct actuals from memory after completion.
4. Work strictly within the approved one-workflow boundary. Record scope deviations and assumptions immediately.
5. Produce each deliverable with explicit labels: SYNTHETIC FACT, CALCULATION, ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS or UNKNOWN.
6. Show every calculation's inputs, formula, units, period and sensitivity. If a baseline is absent, state that impact cannot be quantified.
7. Record tools/models and versions actually used. Use UNKNOWN if not observable; do not infer.
8. Record direct cost as ZERO_CONFIRMED only if verified. Otherwise use UNKNOWN, including for existing/free-tier tools whose inference, usage or allocation cost is not known.
9. Submit the complete output to an independent reviewer who did not produce it.
10. Record every defect, severity, correction, re-review and rework minute.
11. Stop at internal closeout. Do not contact a customer or prospect, publish, price, quote, sell, deploy or deliver externally.

## 7. QA hard stops

The exercise cannot pass if any critical defect remains open. At minimum, critical defects include:
- real or sensitive data entering the synthetic workflow;
- unsupported factual, savings, revenue, ROI or guarantee claims;
- untraceable or materially incorrect calculations;
- scope breach or hidden production access;
- missing evidence for a claimed pass;
- privacy/security or tool-rights issue;
- producer self-approval without independent review;
- unrecorded material scope/method deviation.

Major defects require correction and full independent review of affected sections. Minor defects must be recorded; they cannot be silently omitted.

## 8. Economics record

Capture separately for each scenario:
- producer and reviewer active labour by stage;
- elapsed time and waiting dependencies;
- preparation, administration, QA, corrections and closeout;
- direct cash cost and any inference/software/hosting exposure;
- management-approved shadow labour rate (currently NOT SET);
- rework share and any repeatability differences;
- economic contribution only after all required inputs are approved and evidenced.

Do not count unpaid labour as economically free. Do not infer zero cost from a free-tier label. Do not set or publish a customer price from these two exercises alone.

## 9. Stop / abort conditions

Stop immediately and preserve the internal record if:
- any real personal, customer, financial, credential or confidential data appears;
- the task requires external account access or production changes;
- a tool requires payment, paid API usage, a subscription, trial with payment details or additional credits;
- scope cannot be held to one fictional workflow;
- independent review is unavailable;
- a critical privacy, security, rights, accuracy or integrity concern arises;
- any action would contact a prospect/customer, publish, spend, sign, deploy or otherwise create an external commitment.

Record the stop reason and leave the exercise INCOMPLETE or BLOCKED. Do not work around the stop.
