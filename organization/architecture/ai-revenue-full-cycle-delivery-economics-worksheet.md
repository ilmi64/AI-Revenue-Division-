## 4. Economic formulas

Let:
- (M_d) = direct delivery minutes;
- (M_s) = allocated sales/qualification minutes;
- (M_a) = administration/setup/closeout minutes;
- (M_q) = QA minutes;
- (M_r) = rework/revision minutes;
- (M_u) = included support minutes;
- (R) = owner-approved internal shadow labour rate in CAD/hour;
- (F) = collected fee excluding applicable sales tax;
- (C_p) = payment fees;
- (C_o) = other direct and allocated cash costs.

Then:

[
H_{total} = (M_d + M_s + M_a + M_q + M_r + M_u)/60
]

[
C_{labour} = H_{total} 	imes R
]

[
C_{cash} = C_p + C_o
]

[
Contribution_{before labour} = F - C_{cash}
]

[
Economic contribution = F - C_{cash} - C_{labour}
]

[
Effective hourly contribution = Economic contribution / H_{total}
]

If the company later sets a minimum economic contribution floor (G), the mathematical fee floor before tax is:

[
F_{floor} = C_{cash} + C_{labour} + G
]

This is a cost-derived floor, not proof that a buyer will pay it. Applicable taxes, refunds, payment timing, bad debt and other obligations require separate treatment and qualified advice. Never count sales tax collected as revenue.

## 6. Delivery and quality gates

An internal rehearsal record must include:
- start/end timestamps and actual minutes by phase;
- report version and synthetic input digest;
- calculation review and defect/rework log;
- scope deviations and unresolved unknowns;
- independent QA reviewer and outcome;
- data-minimization and retention closeout;
- repeatability comparison against a second distinct synthetic scenario.

A delivery-time claim is not supportable from one rehearsal. The existing two-rehearsal pricing gate remains in force, and even two synthetic rehearsals do not establish market demand.

## 7. Pricing / commercialization stop gates

Do not publish or quote a price until:
1. Scope and exclusions are stable.
2. At least two authorized synthetic rehearsals have measured full-cycle effort and QA outcomes.
3. Direct and allocated costs are identified.
4. Owner-selected labour-rate and economic-floor assumptions are explicit.
5. Stress-case economics are acceptable.
6. Tax, payment, refund, privacy, contract and data-retention responsibilities are reviewed.
7. A lawful, separately approved validation process provides genuine buyer feedback.
