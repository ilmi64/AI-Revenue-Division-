# Mini-Review Offer Promise-to-Evidence Traceability Matrix

**Status:** INTERNAL DESIGN / NOT REHEARSED / NOT QA-VALIDATED / NOT PRICED / NOT PUBLISHED.  
**Company:** AI Revenue only. **Budget:** CAD $0 incremental.  
**Authority:** Does not authorize rehearsal, prospect contact, customer-data intake, pricing, proposal, publishing or delivery.

## 1. Purpose

Make every customer-facing promise traceable to a deliverable, evidence source, acceptance test and stop condition before any commercial use. The governing offer remains one workflow, one business unit, one decision-maker, with no production access or implementation.

## 2. Promise-to-evidence matrix

| Offer promise / deliverable | Required artifact | Minimum evidence | Acceptance test | Hard stop / unresolved item |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clear one-workflow boundary | Scope and boundary statement | Workflow start/end, business unit, decision owner, exclusions | Reviewer confirms one bounded workflow and no hidden second workflow | Unclear ownership, scope sprawl, production access |
| Current-state workflow map | Step/role/tool/handoff map | Every step linked to evidence or labelled statement/assumption | Traceable sequence; waits and handoffs represented | Missing critical evidence; sensitive data required |
| Evidence-led findings | Atomic claim register | Source/reference, date, evidence class, author, confidence, contrary evidence | Every material factual claim traceable | Untraceable claim or false corroboration |
| Baseline snapshot | Baseline table | Aggregate/redacted input, period, units, denominator, data owner, limitations | Recalculate derived values; label unknowns | No baseline: do not invent benchmark or quantify improvement |
| Friction and risk register | Ranked friction/risk items | Evidence, impact pathway, likelihood/impact rationale, control dependency | Each item traces to a workflow step | Regulated/safety-critical issue outside competence |
| Three prioritized improvements | Recommendation register | Problem link, expected effect, effort, cost, dependency, risk, reversibility | Up to three, ranked transparently; recommendation distinguished from implementation | Paid dependency, unsafe automation, unsupported benefit |
| Conservative impact model | Formula/input/sensitivity sheet | Observed inputs, units, time window, formula, uncertainty | Independent recalculation; sensitivity visible | Missing inputs: mark not quantifiable |
| Practical next-step plan | Action plan | Sequence, accountable role, prerequisites, acceptance criteria, no-cost option if verified | Feasible, bounded, no implied company execution | External action or unapproved paid service |
| Privacy/security note | Data-minimization note | Data classes, collection boundary, access assumptions, retention/deletion | No unnecessary personal/financial/health/credential data | Sensitive data, unclear rights or production access |
| Handover and independent QA | Versioned report and QA record | Reviewer independence, defects, corrections, evidence refs | Critical/major defects closed; correct recipient/version | Producer self-approval or unresolved critical defect |

## 3. Evidence classification

Use explicit labels for every material statement: OBSERVED_INPUT, CLIENT_STATEMENT, CALCULATION, ASSUMPTION, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN or EXTERNAL_REFERENCE. Preserve source and time. Multiple models reading the same input do not create independent evidence. A reasonable recommendation does not make its projected benefit proven.

## 4. Release-blocking QA

Any failure in correct recipient/scope, claim traceability, arithmetic/units, baseline provenance, privacy/security/rights, commercial honesty or independent review blocks release. Scope expansion is not defect correction and requires a new scope decision.

## 5. Time-capture and target reconciliation

The offer specification states a 180-minute target as an unvalidated ceiling. The rehearsal authorization packet and rehearsal plan describe a 210-minute measurement plan. Neither is a validated delivery promise.

Before any authorized rehearsal, the owner/reviewer must choose:
- **A — 210-minute measurement budget:** retain the existing phase plan and treat 180 minutes only as a stretch hypothesis; or
- **B — 180-minute measurement budget:** revise phase allocations and remove/reduce scope transparently before the exercise.

Do not silently compress QA, evidence capture, privacy checks, independent review, administration or closeout. Capture active labour and elapsed waiting separately; full-cycle economics includes acquisition, qualification, proposal, setup, delivery, QA, rework, support, administration and closeout.

## 6. Commercial evidence gates

A synthetic rehearsal tests only the internal method with fictional inputs. It cannot establish buyer demand, willingness to pay, real acquisition cost, production delivery time, customer outcomes, legal/tax/privacy compliance or market fit.

Before pricing or selling: two separately authorized synthetic rehearsals; contemporaneous time/cost capture; independent QA; comparative review; owner-approved shadow labour rate/economic floor; base/stress economics; separately authorized buyer validation; and legal, tax, privacy, contract, retention and payment review.
