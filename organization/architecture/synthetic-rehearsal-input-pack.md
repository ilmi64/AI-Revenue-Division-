## Required cautions
- These figures are deliberately synthetic and must be clearly labelled on every report page.
- The 60 quoted enquiries are a scenario input, not an assumed conversion rate.
- Do not assume every enquiry is eligible for a quote or that automation changes conversion.
- Time saved is not cash saved unless staffing, capacity or opportunity-cost assumptions are explicitly justified.
- Do not monetize time without a management-selected labour rate.
- No claim of customer ROI, savings, increased sales or accuracy can be made from this fictional pack.
