# Research & Intelligence Division — Operating Charter

**Classification:** Organization-level division/shared service; architecture and operating design only  
**Status:** Designed for staged implementation; not staffed or operationally continuous yet  
**Reports to:** Organization-level governance through Main Hub/OCC  
**Serves:** AI Revenue Company, Trading & Technology Company, and future approved divisions through scoped intelligence delivery

## 1. Mandate and boundary

Research & Intelligence is a first-class division of the wider organization. It is not a third operating company and does not merge the two existing companies.

Its mandate is to continuously discover, investigate, verify, preserve and deliver useful intelligence that improves the organization's decisions, competitive position, risk awareness and opportunity discovery.

The division has its own leadership, workforce, task queue, scoped memory, research archives, source registry, quality standards and operating records. It provides research outputs to AI Revenue and Trading & Technology through explicitly scoped interfaces.

It does not own or control either company's executives, finances, customer relationships, products, trading systems, strategy approvals, risk limits or private records. Research recommendations are advisory. The receiving company decides whether and how to use them.

## 2. Leadership and functional roles

The following are target roles. They may initially be covered by a small number of agents/people, but responsibilities and decision boundaries remain explicit.

- **Director of Research & Intelligence / Chief Intelligence Officer:** sets research priorities, quality standards, portfolio balance and delivery commitments; reports organization-level intelligence and escalations.
- **Research Operations Lead:** manages the queue, work allocation, deadlines, shift coverage, handovers, service levels and archive completeness.
- **Markets & Macro Intelligence Lead:** coordinates macro, market structure, cross-asset, Forex, indices, Gold and crypto-market research.
- **Crypto & On-chain Intelligence Lead:** coordinates capital flows, whale/smart-money activity, wallet/entity analysis, launches, unlocks, treasuries and ecosystem flows.
- **Quantitative & Strategy Intelligence Lead:** coordinates strategy hypotheses, bot capability analysis, reverse engineering, independent reproduction, backtesting evidence and validation handoffs.
- **Technology & AI Intelligence Lead:** evaluates models, agent systems, orchestration, runtimes, developer tools, architectures, security and engineering methods.
- **Business & Commercial Intelligence Lead:** researches customer demand, business models, product gaps, pricing, competitors and distribution opportunities for AI Revenue and future businesses.
- **Data & Knowledge Governance Lead:** owns source registration, provenance, timestamps, freshness, deduplication, version history, evidence grades, retention and access labels.
- **Research Quality / Red-Team Lead:** challenges assumptions, checks source reliability, reproduces important findings, records uncertainty and prevents unsupported claims from becoming recommendations.
- **Shift Leads:** maintain continuity between day and night operations and ensure urgent issues reach the correct company authority.

## 3. Specialist research desks and preserved workstreams

The division organizes work into specialist desks. These are workstreams within the division, not additional operating companies.

1. **Grok Desk / HyperGrok research concept** — continuous discovery, monitoring, hypothesis generation, multi-source investigation and challenge/review. Any specific Grok or HyperGrok product access remains subject to availability, privacy, cost and approval checks.
2. **Crypto Capital Flow & Opportunity Intelligence** — funding rounds, token launches, VC/fund activity, whale movements, smart-money sector rotations, launchpad activity, treasury deployments, unlocks/vesting, ecosystem and developer capital flows.
3. **On-chain & Smart-Money Desk** — wallet/entity behaviour, flow attribution, liquidity, holder concentration, exchange/bridge movement and on-chain signals. Preserve J7, Buildix.trade and Sanbase/Santiment as named specialist research sources/workstreams already discussed; verify capabilities and data quality rather than assuming their outputs are correct.
4. **REA / Bot Capability Intelligence** — lawful reverse engineering of public documentation and open-source/authorized targets; capability, architecture, strategy, risk, integration and competitive analysis; convert independently reproducible discoveries into specifications for Trading & Technology.
5. **Quantitative Strategy Intelligence** — research hypotheses, market regimes, entry/exit mechanics, risk models, backtest design, paper validation and evidence quality across crypto, Forex, indices and Gold.
6. **AI & Technology Intelligence** — model/runtime comparisons, agent frameworks, orchestration, memory, evaluation, observability, coding agents, security, open-source capabilities and infrastructure.
7. **AI Business & Product Intelligence** — customer problems, demand signals, competitor offers, product opportunities, service pricing, acquisition channels and distribution for AI Revenue.
8. **Risk & Data Intelligence** — source outages, stale or manipulated data, conflicting evidence, operational risks, market risks, model limitations and emerging threats.
9. **General Organizational Intelligence** — cross-sector developments and approved opportunities relevant to current and future divisions.

Existing research, prior strategy agreements, datasets, registries and findings must be inventoried and consolidated. The division must not discard accumulated work or repeat research without first checking the archive and recording why a refresh is needed.

## 4. Research lifecycle

Every material research item follows a traceable lifecycle:

1. **Intake:** record the question/opportunity, requesting company or organization-level scope, urgency, owner and intended decision use.
2. **Prior-work check:** search the existing archive, strategy registry and source catalogue before starting fresh research.
3. **Plan:** identify hypotheses, source classes, methods, data needs, constraints and stop conditions.
4. **Collect:** gather public, licensed or explicitly authorized information; record exact source, access time, source timestamp and retrieval context.
5. **Analyze:** compare sources, inspect data quality, separate observation from interpretation and record assumptions.
6. **Challenge:** seek disconfirming evidence, alternative explanations, manipulation risks and independent review for high-impact findings.
7. **Grade:** assign evidence class, confidence, freshness and unresolved questions.
8. **Deliver:** produce a company-scoped brief, alert, dataset, technical evaluation or opportunity dossier with clear recommendations and caveats.
9. **Archive:** retain provenance, source references, method, version, evidence, reviewer and supersession history.
10. **Revisit:** refresh time-sensitive findings and record when evidence changes or becomes stale.

## 5. Evidence and confidence standards

Research outputs must distinguish the following states:

- **Observed fact:** directly supported by a primary or reliable source.
- **Corroborated finding:** independently supported by multiple relevant sources.
- **Interpretation:** a reasoned explanation of observed evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** a testable possibility that has not yet been established.
- **Public claim:** a statement made by a project, vendor, influencer or third party; not treated as verified fact.
- **Reproduced mechanic:** independently reconstructed from lawful, available evidence.
- **Backtest result:** output of a documented historical simulation with stated data and methodological limits.
- **Paper-validated result:** independently observed in a non-live simulation or paper environment.
- **Independently validated result:** passed the defined evidence and review requirements for the relevant domain.

Each record carries:
- source identity and source class;
- original publication/update time where available;
- collection time and timezone;
- canonical source reference and archived snapshot/hash where permitted;
- owner, reviewer and version;
- freshness/expiry policy;
- confidence grade and rationale;
- conflicting evidence and unresolved assumptions;
- permitted audience and company scope.

A vendor claim, social post, model-generated answer, simulated result or single-source rumour must never be promoted to verified performance. No strategy is labelled profitable based only on marketing, screenshots, synthetic data or an unreplicated backtest.

## 6. Knowledge base and data governance

The division maintains a structured intelligence archive with separate registries for:
- sources and source reliability;
- research questions and tasks;
- findings, hypotheses and evidence;
- strategies, bots, tools and technical capabilities;
- market events, entities and capital-flow observations;
- customer problems, products, competitors and commercial opportunities;
- risks, incidents, data-quality issues and lessons learned;
- company-scoped deliveries and day/night handovers.

Records are versioned. Corrections and new evidence create a new version or superseding record; historical decisions and prior findings are not silently overwritten.

The archive uses clear access labels:
- public;
- organization-level shared intelligence;
- AI Revenue scoped;
- Trading & Technology scoped;
- restricted / owner-approved.

Public research may be shared across the organization. Company-private information is not copied into the shared research archive by default. A company may provide a limited research question or approved data extract without exposing its complete private records. Any cross-company use of a finding must respect its original scope, licence, confidentiality and approval status.

## 7. Day/night operating model

Continuous day/night coverage is the target operating design, not a claim of current 24/7 operation.

- **Day shift:** intake and prioritization, primary research, company briefings, source verification, specialist collaboration and scheduled reviews.
- **Night shift:** monitoring, alert triage, research continuation, independent challenge, time-sensitive opportunity tracking and preparation of morning handovers.
- **Shift handover:** outgoing lead records open tasks, completed findings, source freshness, confidence, unresolved issues, alerts, next actions and the receiving owner. The incoming lead acknowledges ownership.
- **Escalation:** urgent market, security, data-integrity or commercial intelligence is routed to the appropriate company authority. Research staff may alert and recommend; they cannot execute company actions or override authority.
- **Coverage stages:** begin with defined research windows and tested handovers; expand coverage only when staffing, reliability, monitoring and cost controls justify it. Do not claim continuous coverage until it is demonstrably staffed/automated and rehearsed.

## 8. Service catalogue and delivery interfaces

The division provides:
- scheduled intelligence briefings and market/technology watch reports;
- urgent alerts with evidence and confidence labels;
- opportunity dossiers with upside, uncertainty, costs, risks and validation steps;
- strategy and bot capability dossiers for Trading & Technology;
- data-source assessments and provenance/quality reports;
- AI model, agent, framework and tooling evaluations;
- customer, competitor, pricing and product-demand reports for AI Revenue;
- risk and red-team reviews;
- historical research retrieval and decision-support packages.

Every delivery identifies its intended recipient, permitted scope, date, freshness, confidence, evidence links, assumptions and owner. Trading & Technology receives trading-relevant intelligence through its own approved intake; AI Revenue receives commercial and technology intelligence through its own approved intake. Neither company gains access to the other's private records through Research & Intelligence.

## 9. Tooling and workforce constraints

- Use the shared model-agnostic infrastructure only through company/division-specific identities, contexts, queues and permissions.
- Research agents must not share a single unrestricted identity across the two companies.
- Paperclip and Ruflo remain leading architecture candidates, not confirmed installed or operational systems.
- Model/runtime choices must be verified for compatibility, licensing, privacy, cost, compute and data handling.
- REA remains research/preparation only until separately approved for installation or execution; lawful public/open-source/authorized research only.
- No credential harvesting, access-control bypass, proprietary source theft, unauthorized exploitation or collection of private account secrets.
- No paid subscriptions, APIs, credits, hosting or trials with billing risk without explicit owner approval.
- No installations, external connections, publishing, customer contact, trading, spending or other consequential external actions without the required separate approval.

## 10. Readiness and implementation gates

The division is not operational merely because this charter exists. Its staged readiness gates are:

1. **D0 — Inventory:** preserve prior research, source lists, strategy work and existing registries.
2. **D1 — Governance:** approve role boundaries, access labels, evidence grading, archive/version policy and escalation rules.
3. **D2 — Knowledge base:** establish scoped registries and traceable research records using approved no-cost infrastructure.
4. **D3 — Workforce:** define specialist agent identities, queues, evaluation and human/owner review points.
5. **D4 — Handover:** rehearse day/night ownership transfer, alert escalation and archive continuity.
6. **D5 — Company interfaces:** prove that each company receives only its own approved intelligence outputs and cannot access the other's private records.
7. **D6 — Continuous operations:** demonstrate reliable coverage, source freshness monitoring, incident handling, backup/restore and cost controls before describing the service as continuous.

Research & Intelligence can be built in parallel with both companies. Its unfinished implementation must not become a launch prerequisite for either standalone company; each company may use approved public sources and its own research capability while the shared division is being completed.
