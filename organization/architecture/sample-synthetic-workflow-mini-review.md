# Synthetic Mini-Review — Northstar Home Services

<badge>Fictional example — invented figures</badge>

**Status:** Internal demonstration only. No actual business, customer or performance claim.

## 1. Scope

One workflow: incoming service enquiry to complete intake record. The fictional business receives enquiries by email and phone, then manually records them in a spreadsheet.

## 2. Problem statement

The fictional owner says staff sometimes re-enter details and chase missing information. This is a fictional client statement, not observed evidence.

## 3. Baseline

| Measure | Synthetic value | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Enquiries per month | 30 | Invented assumption |
| Intake record time | 6 minutes each | Invented assumption |
| Monthly intake recording time | 180 minutes / 3 hours | Calculated from invented inputs |
| Missing-information rate | Unknown | Not estimated |
| Lost leads / revenue impact | Unknown | Not estimated |

Calculation: 30 × 6 minutes = 180 minutes. This is the current workload assumption, not a forecast of recoverable time.

## 4. Three opportunities

| Rank | Opportunity | Why first | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standardize required intake fields | Low-risk process clarification before buying tools | Low |
| 2 | Use one approved lead-tracking record format | May reduce duplicate entry if duplication is confirmed | Low–medium |
| 3 | Consider automatic form-to-tracker transfer | Only after field definitions and privacy review | Medium; not part of this mini-review |

## 5. Recommendation

Start with a one-page intake checklist and a named owner for each enquiry. Observe a small sample for two weeks and measure completion time, missing fields and duplicate records. Only then decide whether an automation is justified.

The business, volumes and timings are fictional. No customer willingness to pay, savings, revenue uplift, automation feasibility or implementation cost has been demonstrated.

**Demonstration outcome:** shows a short evidence-led review; does not prove the package is commercially validated or can meet the three-hour delivery target.
