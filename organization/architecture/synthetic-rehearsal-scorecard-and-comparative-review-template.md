## 8. Comparative repeatability review

After both exercises are separately authorized and completed, compare:
- Scope adherence and unplanned scope expansion.
- Active effort by stage and role.
- Elapsed time and waiting dependencies.
- First-pass QA and defect severity.
- Rework causes and correction effort.
- Evidence completeness and calculation reproducibility.
- Tool/model dependence and cost uncertainty.
- Differences caused by scenario complexity rather than delivery-method inconsistency.

Explain material differences; do not average away a critical failure. Two synthetic rehearsals can provide limited internal process evidence only. They do not validate customer demand, willingness to pay, production delivery time, profitability, legal compliance or market fit.
