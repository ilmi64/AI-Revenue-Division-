## 1. Purpose
Preserve researched business models without allowing speculative opportunities to displace the first revenue-ready service or consume capital before revenue exists. An entry is not an approved business, offer, supplier, purchase, subscription, customer commitment or forecast.

Current execution priority remains the practical operations / workflow improvement service.

## 2. Future opportunity A — AI-enabled recurring marketing agency

**Origin:** Research into the general model promoted by Amanah Growth Partners / Amanah Advisory. The reusable idea is a recurring service business helping small businesses attract, convert and follow up with customers through repeatable marketing and operational systems. This is not an endorsement of that provider, its programme, claims or results.

Potential future services:
- Campaign planning and content calendars.
- AI-assisted content and creative production.
- Website / landing-page conversion improvements.
- Lead capture, qualification and follow-up workflows.
- SEO and local-search improvement.
- Reporting and monthly optimization.
- Carefully scoped marketing automation and CRM workflow support.

The potential revenue mechanism is a clearly scoped monthly retainer for continuing delivery, measurement and improvement—not guaranteed leads, revenue or a particular monthly income.

**Why it may fit later:** it could reuse AI Revenue capabilities in creative production, websites, research, workflow automation and reporting. A service-first model may precede proprietary software, provided delivery capacity and economics are proven.

**Activation gates:**
1. Select a narrow customer segment and measurable problem.
2. Verify demand and willingness to pay through an owner-approved validation process.
3. Define monthly deliverables, exclusions, service levels, revision limits and client responsibilities.
4. Measure delivery, review, software, subcontracting, support, rework and acquisition effort.
5. Prove quality and retention through separately approved pilot work.
6. Review privacy, consent, anti-spam, advertising standards, IP, platform rules and Canadian/Ontario obligations.
7. Screen client sectors and campaigns for Shafi'i/halal concerns; refer disputed matters for qualified review.
8. Prohibit fabricated testimonials, deceptive claims, undisclosed synthetic endorsements and guaranteed outcomes.
9. Confirm customer control of accounts/data, portability, termination, access revocation and deletion.
10. Assess profitability after labour and support, not merely recurring billing.

**Halal / ethical issue register:** “Halal marketing” is not self-certifying. Review each client, product, campaign, contract, payment arrangement, data source, consent basis, subcontractor and deliverable. Exclude prohibited or materially questionable sectors and deceptive practices. Do not issue religious rulings; seek qualified Shafi'i advice where needed.

**Disposition: DEFERRED — RECORD ONLY.** No programme purchase, coaching, paid training, payment details, contact, account creation, advertising spend, client outreach, public claims or contractual commitment is authorized. Provider claims of $10K/month or 90-day outcomes are not evidence of achievable results.

## 3. Future opportunity B — capital-light liquidation / resale intelligence

Preserve the service-first model before owning inventory:
- Manifest normalization and completeness checks.
- Product identification and condition-risk classification from authorized information.
- Conservative sold-comparable research.
- Fee, shipping, tax and return-adjusted resale-recovery estimates.
- Listing data preparation and inventory spreadsheets.
- Lot-level decision reports for prospective buyers or resellers.

Inventory ownership, pallet bids and purchases remain deferred until AI Revenue has real collected revenue, sufficient reserve capital and an approved total-cost case.

Activation gates: lawful access and reuse of manifests/photos/marketplace data; repeatable identification and comparable methodology; conservative net recovery including buyer premiums, freight, HST, platform/payment fees, returns, defects, storage and disposal; measured delivery time and willingness to pay; clear condition/counterfeit/recall/restricted-goods controls.

**Disposition: DEFERRED — CAPITAL-LIGHT SERVICE RESEARCH ONLY.** No supplier/customer contact, inventory, bid, purchase or listing is authorized.
