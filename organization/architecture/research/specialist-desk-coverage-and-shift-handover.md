# Research & Intelligence — Specialist Desk Coverage & Shift Handover

**Status:** Coverage design and operating templates; no staffed 24/7 service is claimed.  
**Accountable owner:** Director of Research & Intelligence.  
**Operations owner:** Research Operations Lead.  
**Scope:** Shared organization-level research service supporting two independent recipient companies.

## 1. Operating principle

The division is designed for continuous day/night research, but coverage is earned through named accountable ownership, approved access, reliable queues, monitoring, escalation and rehearsed handovers. A schedule or agent prompt does not prove that a desk is staffed or functioning.

Begin with bounded research windows and explicit on-call ownership. Expand hours only after coverage, failure handling, cost and handover evidence support the change. If no qualified owner is available, mark the desk **UNCOVERED** and defer or escalate; never fabricate monitoring.

## 3. Coverage levels and status vocabulary

- **PLANNED:** design exists; no operational coverage claim.
- **WINDOWED:** a defined research window has a named accountable owner.
- **COVERED:** owner, approved sources, queue, escalation and monitoring are currently available and evidenced.
- **DEGRADED:** partial source, staffing, model, queue or monitoring failure limits coverage.
- **UNCOVERED:** no accountable coverage; missed window recorded.
- **HANDOVER_PENDING:** outgoing record exists but incoming owner has not acknowledged.
- **HANDOVER_ACCEPTED:** incoming owner accepted task ownership and unresolved issues.
- **PAUSED:** source, security, policy, cost or governance stop condition prevents work.

Never use “24/7”, “live”, “continuous” or “always monitoring” unless independently supported by coverage rosters, telemetry, source-health evidence, escalation tests and completed shift handovers over a defined observation period.

## 5. Priority and escalation

- **P0 — Immediate integrity/security event:** suspected compromised source/account, secret exposure, unauthorized access, data poisoning, unexpected billing or cross-company data leak. Stop affected work/publication, preserve evidence and alert the designated security/owner authority immediately.
- **P1 — Time-sensitive material event:** major market, protocol, regulatory, funding, source or product event with a narrow relevance window. Verify before alerting; state confidence and alternative explanations.
- **P2 — Scheduled research:** planned desk deliverable, source review, comparison or recurring briefing.
- **P3 — Backlog/enrichment:** non-urgent archive cleanup, broad discovery and low-impact context.

Research staff may pause their own work, quarantine a source and notify the responsible authority. They cannot place trades, move funds, change risk limits, authorize spending, contact customers, publish externally or override company executives.

Escalations must state: what happened; when observed; affected source/desk/company; evidence; confidence; impact; action already taken within authority; owner required; next update time. If the recipient is unavailable, record the escalation as unacknowledged and follow the approved fallback chain; do not assume delivery.

## 6. Mandatory shift handover record

Every outgoing shift creates a durable record with:

- handover ID, date, timezone, coverage window and outgoing/incoming owners;
- desks covered, degraded, paused or uncovered;
- active task IDs, priorities, deadlines, status and next action;
- new findings and report IDs with recipient scope and evidence grade;
- source freshness exceptions, outages, methodology/terms changes and source holds;
- contradictions, alternative explanations and unresolved attribution;
- incidents, escalations, recipients, acknowledgement and next update deadline;
- blocked work and exact unblock condition;
- cost/resource status and any unexpected usage;
- decisions required from a company owner (clearly distinguished from research recommendations);
- archive/version updates and recovery notes;
- incoming acknowledgement, acceptance timestamp and rejected/unaccepted items.

No raw company-private content is copied into the shared handover. Use the minimum scoped task reference and publish only approved summaries.

## 7. Handover acceptance checklist

Incoming lead must explicitly confirm:

1. I am the named accountable owner for this window.
2. I can access only the approved identity, queues and source classes.
3. I have reviewed all P0/P1 events and outstanding escalations.
4. I understand stale, blocked, contradictory and unverified sources.
5. I accept each task transferred to me, or have returned it with a reason.
6. I know the fallback and incident contacts.
7. I have not inferred that a missed window was covered.
8. I have recorded any desk that remains uncovered.

If no acknowledgement arrives by the handover deadline, status remains **HANDOVER_PENDING** and the Operations Lead is alerted. No silent ownership transfer.

## 8. Desk-specific minimum daily checklist

- **Markets desks:** exact instrument/product form; venue/chain; market session/timezone; source timestamp; spread/fees/funding/settlement caveats; data gaps; halal issue flags where applicable.
- **Capital-flow desk:** event vs interpretation; amount/currency/date; primary announcement or transaction evidence; wallet attribution grade; internal transfer/OTC/custody alternatives; unlock vs claim vs sale distinction.
- **Quant desk:** registered hypothesis ID; frozen definitions; data source/version; chronological split and leakage controls; costs/slippage assumptions; falsification status; no unsupported profitability claim.
- **REA/technology desk:** lawful target and access basis; official docs/licence; capability vs marketing claim; runtime/dependency/cost/security; reproduction status; no installation or execution without separate approval.
- **AI commercial desk:** source date and geography; advertised price vs verified transaction; competitor scope; customer-demand evidence vs anecdote; no outreach or commitments.
- **Risk/data desk:** source health and freshness; terms/identity changes; conflicting evidence; security/privacy/jurisdiction; incident status and quarantine.
- **Grok Desk / HyperGrok:** generated leads are hypotheses until source-checked; preserve prompt/model provenance where material; independent challenge; no model-generated fact accepted without evidence.

## 9. Coverage metrics (report honestly)

Track per desk and per shift:

- planned vs covered windows;
- named owner and reviewer availability;
- handover completion and acknowledgement rate;
- P0/P1 detection-to-triage and escalation-to-acknowledgement times;
- queue age, overdue tasks and dead-letter count;
- percentage of material claims with primary evidence and independent challenge;
- stale-source rate and source outage duration;
- unresolved contradiction and attribution counts;
- correction/supersession rate;
- source/agent failures and recovery time;
- unexpected cost or rate-limit events;
- company-scope publication errors (target zero).

Do not optimize for volume of alerts or reports. Quality, freshness, low false-positive rate, useful coverage and safe boundaries matter more.

## 10. Continuity and degraded-mode rules

- Model/provider outage: pause affected tasks or move to a separately approved fallback; preserve context and do not broaden permissions.
- Orchestrator/queue outage: use approved manual handover record; do not create an untracked parallel queue.
- Source outage or stale data: mark stale, use an independently verified alternative if permitted, otherwise hold.
- Reviewer unavailable: defer high-impact publication; low-risk work may remain draft.
- Identity/tenant uncertainty: stop access and publication until resolved.
- Cost or free-tier limit reached: stop calls; no automatic upgrade, paid overage or key substitution.
- Cross-company scope uncertainty: quarantine the report until the recipient and data boundary are clear.
- No accountable shift owner: mark UNCOVERED and record missed coverage.

## 11. Readiness gates for expanding coverage

1. **Roster:** named accountable lead, backup, desk owners and escalation chain.
2. **Identity:** separate Research tenant identities, least privilege and revocation.
3. **Queues:** durable task state, deadlines, cancellation, retry and dead-letter handling.
4. **Sources:** completed source scorecards, terms/licence checks, freshness and outage detection.
5. **Quality:** evidence grades, independent challenge and correction history.
6. **Publication:** recipient-specific review, redaction and authenticated delivery.
7. **Handover:** tabletop and operational rehearsal with missed-shift and unavailable-owner scenarios.
8. **Continuity:** backup/restore, provider outage and incident recovery evidence.
9. **Economics:** measured resource use within the no-spend policy and explicit stop controls.
10. **Assurance:** independent review and evidence-backed coverage period before any continuous-service claim.

## 13. 2026-11-21 shared day/night coverage and recipient-scoped handover

This shared Research & Intelligence coverage design supports both independent recipient companies. It does not create a shared company tenant, shared treasury, shared customer record or shared decision authority.

### 13.1 Day/night operating pattern

- **Day coverage:** scheduled work-order intake, primary-source verification, commercial/product research, methodology review, recipient-specific publication preparation and owner escalations.
- **Night coverage / Grok Desk and HyperGrok:** bounded cross-time-zone discovery, source-health review, event triage, contradiction search and draft synthesis under the same source, identity, cost and publication controls.
- **Independent challenge:** remains a distinct review function. A night synthesis is not self-approved, and multiple model outputs using the same upstream evidence are not independent corroboration.
- **Recipient separation:** AI Revenue and Trading & Technology tasks retain separate company IDs, data classifications, approved source scopes, publication envelopes and audit trails. Shared R&I infrastructure does not permit cross-company reads or copying raw private content into a common handover.
- **Uncovered periods:** if no accountable owner, approved source access, reliable queue or escalation route is available, mark UNCOVERED or PAUSED. A model, prompt, scheduled task or generated report is not evidence of staffed coverage.

### 13.2 Mandatory shared handover envelope

Every shift transition records:
- handover ID, UTC timestamp, named outgoing/incoming lead, timezone and coverage window;
- R&I work-order IDs, desk, priority, deadline, owner, reviewer and status;
- recipient company ID and publication scope for every material output;
- source registry revision, source health, last event/retrieval timestamps, rights state and quarantine flags;
- evidence IDs, claim confidence, counter-evidence, source-dependence and unresolved contradictions;
- incident/escalation IDs, severity, recipient, acknowledgement and next update deadline;
- queue watermark, retries, dead-letter status and cancellation/revocation state;
- resource/cost status, free-tier limits and stop conditions;
- archive revision, correction/supersession links and next actions.

Only minimum scoped references may be carried in the shared operations envelope. Company-private payloads stay in the originating company's tenant and are retrieved only by an authorized recipient identity.

### 13.3 Recipient-specific handover boundaries

| Recipient | Permitted handover summary | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| AI Revenue | Its own offer, customer-demand, product, competitor, delivery, economics and operational work orders | No Trading positions, account data, strategy details or private capital-flow intelligence unless separately approved and lawfully sanitized |
| Trading & Technology | Its own market, instrument, source, quant, risk and technology research references | No AI Revenue customer records, commercial pipeline, private economics or credentials |
| Shared R&I operations | Work-order metadata, coverage status, source governance and sanitized cross-company service-health metrics | No pooled private datasets, shared memory authority or cross-tenant publication |

### 13.4 Coverage status remains evidence-based

| Capability | Status |
|---|---|

| Grok Desk / HyperGrok workflow | DESIGN_PRESENT / NOT_OPERATIONAL |

| Persistent queue, source monitoring and alerting | NOT_CONFIGURED |

**No false-coverage rule:** continuous coverage is an architectural target only. Do not claim staffed shifts, live monitoring, autonomous night operations or successful handovers until named ownership, approved access, persistent queues, monitoring, escalation, recovery and independently assessed rehearsals are evidenced.
