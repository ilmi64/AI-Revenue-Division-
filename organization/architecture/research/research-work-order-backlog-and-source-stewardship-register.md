## 2. Priority

P0: security, privacy, cross-tenant, integrity or unexpected-cost incident.  
P1: narrow-window event that may materially affect a near-term decision; verification is never bypassed.  
P2: important planned work with a clear decision use.  
P3: exploratory or low-urgency work.

Priority is not evidence confidence or authority to act.

## 4. Evidence and challenge

Claim grades: PRIMARY_CONFIRMED, CORROBORATED, SINGLE_SOURCE, VENDOR_ESTIMATE, PUBLIC_CLAIM, ATTRIBUTED, HYPOTHESIS, CONTRADICTED, SUPERSEDED.

Record claim-level evidence separately from source quality. Challenge exact entity/product/version, publication date, methodology, independent corroboration, selection bias, missing data, alternative explanations, terms, privacy and recipient scope.

## 5. Queue and publication safeguards

One publication has one recipient. If both companies need a finding, create two separately reviewed reports. OCC receives allowlisted aggregate metadata only. No raw customer records, private strategies, credentials, full report bodies or cross-company memory.
