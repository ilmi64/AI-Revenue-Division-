# Technology Intelligence & REA Capability Discovery Framework

**Status:** Organization-wide research mandate; REA remains a candidate tool, not installed or integrated.
**Owner:** Research & Intelligence Division, with recipient-specific work orders from each company.
**Authority:** Advisory only.

## 1. Mandate

Discover, understand and evaluate technologies, applications, bots, agents, business models and workflows that could materially improve either operating company. REA (Reverse Engineer Anything MCP) is a priority specialist capability for lawful analysis and capability extraction. Its use is not confined to trading.

R&I maintains the shared technology intelligence process and public/authorized evidence registry. AI Revenue and Trading & Technology each retain separate product, security, risk, budget and adoption decisions. R&I cannot deploy or authorize a tool for either company.

## 3. REA investigation workflow

1. Authorize the target: identify public or explicitly authorized target, permitted scope and purpose. Reject targets requiring credential access, access-control bypass, private datasets or unauthorized exploitation.
2. Establish baseline: document our existing capability, limitations, costs, reliability and acceptance criteria.
3. Inspect public documentation, public interfaces, open-source code and lawful observable behaviour. Record source and revision.
4. Decompose user journeys, functional modules, inputs/outputs, state transitions, interfaces, data flows, dependencies and failure handling.
5. Verify provenance and rights: inspect licence, terms, attribution and relevant restrictions. Review dependencies, permissions, network and filesystem exposure.
6. Extract capability, not protected expression: identify general methods, requirements, interface contracts and independently implementable ideas. Do not copy proprietary code, protected assets, hidden server logic or private datasets.
7. Reproduce safely: create an isolated design or sandbox plan using synthetic/public data; no production credentials, funds, customer records or live execution.
8. Compare parity, reliability, security, performance, maintainability, cost and compatibility against the baseline.
9. Independent reviewer challenges evidence, assumptions, licence and security findings.
10. Recommend adopt, integrate, adapt, independently build, monitor, defer or reject.
11. Publish recipient-scoped report; a report to one company is not automatically shared with the other.
12. Record implementation outcome, regressions, savings, quality and follow-up research.

## 4. REA capability record

Each investigation stores target ID/name/category/developer and canonical source; requester, recipient scope and authorization; retrieval date, source revision, licence and terms; target purpose/users/workflows; capability inventory and architecture/interface map; observed behaviour and limitations; dependencies, data flows, permissions and security risks; cost/runtime/platform/compatibility; candidate improvements and independent reproduction status; adoption decision, rationale, reviewer, owner and next review date.

Keep original source evidence separate from derived analysis. Preserve contradictory observations and uncertainty. Vendor claims or reverse-engineered diagrams are not proof of performance.

## 6. AI Revenue application

Use the same discovery discipline to examine successful business workflows and applications relevant to opportunity discovery and customer-problem validation; website/service production and workflow automation; content/creative generation and distribution; sales operations, support and delivery quality; product onboarding, retention and customer experience; cost-effective agent workflows and internal tooling.

Output a commercially testable improvement proposal: target customer/problem, observed workflow, value hypothesis, rights/security assessment, smallest independent build, cost ceiling, quality criteria and evidence needed. No customer contact, publishing, purchases or commitments without owner approval.

## 7. REA operational constraints

REA repository morluto/rea is a research candidate. It is not installed, tested or connected. Do not install or execute it, modify its own repository, spend money or integrate it with PolyBot until a supported Windows/Mac/Linux runtime is available and the user explicitly authorizes the specific action. Android-only preparation does not bypass this requirement.

Public-source research and architecture planning may continue. Any later execution must be isolated, least-privilege, reviewed for licence/security and use synthetic/public data. No credentials, secrets, private customer data, proprietary source extraction or unrestricted network/filesystem permissions.

## 8. Success criteria

REA is useful only when it produces independently reviewed capability discoveries that improve measurable outcomes: shorter time-to-capability, lower operating cost, better reliability, stronger security, improved research quality, higher delivery quality or better validated commercial/trading workflows. Number of targets inspected, diagrams created or tools discovered is not a success metric by itself.
