## 3. Work-order cards

### RI-2026-11-21-AR-DEMAND-01 — Zero-upfront demand and acquisition paths

- **Recipient:** AI_REVENUE only.
- **Question:** Which lawful, no-upfront-cost service/product acquisition routes have the strongest evidence of reachable Canadian buyers, credible demand, clear delivery boundaries and a path to cleared payment?
- **Decision relevance:** Prioritize the first viable AI Revenue revenue path; do not allow bounty scarcity to block service, software, automation or digital-product routes.
- **Scope:** Publicly available demand signals, public procurement/opportunity notices, public project requests, platform eligibility and payout documentation, competitor offer positioning, buyer problem evidence, and no-cost distribution constraints.
- **Exclusions:** No outreach, account creation, platform registration, bidding, claims, comments, code submission, customer data, paid tools, paid APIs, scraping contrary to terms, or commitments.
- **Allowed source classes:** PRIMARY_OFFICIAL, ESTABLISHED_REPORTING, OPEN_SOURCE, COMMUNITY (clearly labelled), INTERNAL_EXPERIMENT (synthetic only).
- **Required outputs:** Ranked opportunity ledger; exact buyer/problem/need; evidence and source freshness; Canadian eligibility/payment route; cost and platform constraints; delivery capability fit; counter-evidence; next owner decision.
- **Acceptance:** Every top-ranked route has a traceable source, clear status (open/closed/uncertain), no-upfront cost assessment, eligibility/payment evidence, realistic delivery requirement, and explicit unresolved risks. No forecast is reported as revenue.
- **Proposed lead:** AI Business / Product / Customer Demand.
- **Independent challenge:** R&I Evidence & Reproducibility.
- **Stop conditions:** Any required payment, paid API, trial with payment details, unclear rights, prohibited outreach, uncertain eligibility or external action requirement.

### RI-2026-11-21-AR-DELIVERY-02 — Mini-Review delivery readiness and economics evidence

- **Recipient:** AI_REVENUE only.
- **Question:** What evidence must be collected in two separately authorized synthetic rehearsals before the Mini-Review can be priced or sold responsibly?
- **Decision relevance:** Prevent unsupported pricing, overstated delivery-time claims and unverified service quality.
- **Scope:** Static comparison of the existing offer specification, QA/time-capture standard, rehearsal scenarios, authorization packet, economics worksheet and scorecard. Identify field-level inconsistencies and exact measurement requirements.
- **Exclusions:** Do not execute either rehearsal; do not contact buyers; do not create real customer records; no paid systems, external delivery, pricing publication or commercial commitment.
- **Required outputs:** Traceability matrix from offer promise to acceptance test; 180-minute target versus 210-minute rehearsal-plan discrepancy; per-step time capture; defect/rework categories; reviewer independence; evidence retention; full-cycle cost/economic-floor inputs; authorization checklist.
- **Acceptance:** No unresolved contradiction is hidden; every offer promise has a test/evidence field; every unknown economic input is explicitly marked unmeasured; no price is recommended until two authorized synthetic rehearsals, independent QA, buyer validation and legal/tax/privacy/payment review.
- **Proposed lead:** AI Revenue Operations / CFO.
- **Independent challenge:** Separate QA reviewer not authoring the rehearsal output.
- **Stop conditions:** Any request to run the rehearsal without explicit authorization, use real customer data, incur cost or publish a price.

### RI-2026-11-21-TT-SOURCE-03 — Multi-market source and instrument qualification

- **Recipient:** TRADING_TECHNOLOGY only.
- **Question:** For Crypto/PolyBot, Forex, Indices and Gold, which exact instrument/product identities, source entitlements, terms, calendars, data semantics and freshness rules remain unqualified?
- **Decision relevance:** Prevent invalid market observations, mixed product semantics, unlicensed use and false signal readiness.
- **Scope:** Documentation and public official-source research only. Maintain separate market-specific rows; preserve Gold feature-chain artifacts and existing provider/product uncertainty. Distinguish exchange/provider instruments from broker CFD products and index references.
- **Exclusions:** No live feeds, API keys, authenticated account access, provider sign-up, purchases, market orders, wallet access, paper signals or strategy performance claims.
- **Allowed source classes:** PRIMARY_OFFICIAL, DIRECT_DATASET, ESTABLISHED_REPORTING, VENDOR (terms clearly reviewed), INTERNAL_EXPERIMENT.
- **Required outputs:** Per-market qualification matrix with canonical identity, venue/provider, native symbol, product type, contract/multiplier, quote/base currency, tick/lot/precision, session/calendar/timezone, timestamp/finality/revision/gap semantics, terms/rights, freshness proposal, provenance and unresolved questions.
- **Acceptance:** No source is called approved until exact terms, rights, product identity and technical semantics are documented and reviewed by Market Data, relevant desk lead, Quant, CRO and Security as applicable. Unknowns remain blockers.
- **Proposed lead:** Market Data & Source Integrity.
- **Independent challenge:** Quant/independent source reviewer; CRO review for risk-sensitive implications.
- **Stop conditions:** Unclear product identity, stale/ambiguous source terms, paid access, missing legal/halal review or any execution request.

## 4. Shared execution and evidence rules

1. Intake and triage must assign a unique research ID, one recipient, accountable desk, independent challenger, bounded question, exclusions, deadline and acceptance criteria.
2. Source discovery creates a CANDIDATE record only. Terms and permitted use must be reviewed before the source is used for substantive work.
3. Every material statement must be an atomic claim linked to exact evidence captures. Preserve original publication time and retrieval time separately.
4. Distinguish direct observation, inference, hypothesis, forecast and opinion. Record contrary evidence and source dependencies.
5. Independent challenge must be performed by a reviewer separate from the author. Multiple models using the same upstream source do not count as independent corroboration.
6. Publish separately to each recipient. A shared public fact may be reused only after recipient-specific scope and sanitisation checks.
7. OCC receives minimal read-only status metadata, never the full confidential report or authority to change work.
8. Expired or contradicted claims are marked and superseded; history is not silently overwritten.
9. If sources, staffing, terms or budget are unavailable, mark BLOCKED and continue another independent work order.
10. Every output states whether it is PREPARED, RESEARCHED, CHALLENGED, APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION or PUBLISHED. Do not infer later states.
